Solmara is a digital audio workstation: you play, record and arrange music in it, shape the sound with instruments and effects, and mix and master the result. This manual shows you how.

<Aside type="caution" title="Solmara is early">
Solmara is in active development and changes from week to week. This manual is new too: the pages below are the first ones, and more are on the way.
</Aside>

## How to

- [Tracks](guides/tracks/): add, name, colour, group and freeze them.
- [Work with clips](guides/clips/): make, move, resize, loop and split them.
- [Write notes in the piano roll](guides/piano-roll/): draw, edit, quantize, velocity and controllers.
- [Compose with chords](guides/composer/): chords, keys, sequences, voicing and layers in Composer.
- [Drum and step patterns](guides/drum-patterns/): beats and basslines, a step at a time.
- [Devices and presets](guides/devices/): instruments, effects, presets, racks and plugins.
- [Automate a sound](guides/automation/): make knobs move as the song plays.
- [Audio clips](guides/audio-clips/): import, fade, line up, split into stems.
- [Record](guides/recording/): notes and audio, takes and comps.
- [Mix](guides/mixing/): the mixer, sends, groups, the master and mastering.
- [The library and sounds](guides/library/): find, keep and get more sounds.
- [Save and export](guides/projects/): projects, templates, rendering and MIDI.
