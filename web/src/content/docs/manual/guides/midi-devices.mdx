---
title: MIDI devices
description: Arpeggios, chords, basslines, beats and note tools that play or change the notes before the instrument.
---

**MIDI devices** sit before a track's instrument and play notes of their own, or change the notes they are given: an arpeggio from a held chord, a bassline from one key, a beat from nothing. Several can stand in a row, each taking what the one before it gave. They are in the library under **MIDI effects**.

Their color says their job: periwinkle for **harmony**, green for **arpeggios and lines**, ember for **rhythm**, lilac for the **tools** that change notes.

## Harmony

| Device | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Key Chords** | Keeps notes in a scale, and plays each as a chord of it. |
| **Chord Pads** | Sixteen chord pads, from sets in many styles or written by hand, and a progression to play them. |
| **Chord Steps** | A step sequencer of chords, arpeggios and strums, kept in a scale. |

## Arpeggios and lines

| Device | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Arpeggiator** | Plays the notes you hold in a shape (up, down, as played, as a chord, at random) or a pattern you draw. |
| **Twin Arpeggiator** | Two arpeggiators side by side, each over its own range of keys. |
| **Anchor Arpeggiator** | An anchor on the chord's notes and a line that moves around it. |
| **Bassline Maker** | A bassline of an on-beat and an off-beat phrase, varied at a click. |
| **Euclid Lines** | Four lines of Euclidean rhythms whose notes wander in a scale. |
| **Pattern Variations** | A pattern of steps, and variations made of it at a click. |

## Rhythm

| Device | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Drum Grid** | Eight channels of sixteen steps, each a drum, eight patterns. |
| **Rhythm Map** | A map of rhythms: move across it and the beat changes, thinned or filled by density. |

## Note tools

| Device | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Note Repeater** | Repeats each note like a delay, its velocity and pitch moving as it goes, for echoes, strums and stacked chords. |
| **Note Filter** | Lets through the notes you want, and changes their pitch, length and velocity. |
| **Note Chance** | Lets notes through by a pattern or by chance, and varies them. |
| **Transpose** | Moves the notes by steps of a scale, by semitones and by octaves. |

## Playing them

- A device that plays a pattern runs **with the song**, from its start, or waits for **a key** you hold, as its section says. Played from a keyboard, it follows you live.
- Each has presets to start from: grooves, melodies, chord progressions and echo shapes.
- Its choices by chance are the same every time you play, so a render sounds as you heard it.

## Turn it into notes

**Send notes to track**, in a MIDI device's menu, writes what the track's MIDI devices play, over the loop or the whole song, into a new clip of plain notes, and bypasses the devices, so it sounds the same and can now be edited note by note.
