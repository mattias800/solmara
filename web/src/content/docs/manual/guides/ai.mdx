---
title: AI on your own computer
description: Generate songs and parts, write phrases in Composer, split stems, turn audio into notes, and make a cover.
---

Solmara's AI runs on your own computer, on its graphics card where it has one. Nothing you make is sent anywhere, and everything a model makes is an ordinary part of your project: a take is an audio clip, a phrase is notes, a stem is a track.

## The models

Each model downloads the first time you use it. **Preferences › AI** lists them with their description, source, licence and size: **Download model** fetches one, and **Check model** checks an installed one and repairs it. **Run on** chooses the processor or a graphics card.

## Generate music on a track

1. Add an **audio track**, right-click its row and choose **Create AI generation clip**. Drag it as long as the music should be; a clip made without dragging is 16 bars.
2. The clip's panel has three columns:
   - **What to make:** describe the music in words, and set its key, whether it is **instrumental** or **sung** (and in which language), and the quality: **Fast**, the model you chose, or **Best**.
   - **Lyrics**, for a sung take: sections tagged `[Verse 1]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, each in a colour of its own.
   - **Takes:** every take you make, newest first.
3. Press **Generate**. Each press makes a new take; you can keep working and playing while it does.
4. Click a take to put it in the clip, and its star to keep it. <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> play the take above or below; <kbd>Enter</kbd> puts it in the clip.

### Make it fit your song

**From the project**, under the music, lets a take start from what you have:

- **Continue the music before it:** the take carries on from what the tracks you choose play before the clip.
- **Accompany tracks with a part:** a part made to fit what your tracks play: vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keys, strings and more.
- **Take tracks as a reference of style:** the take borrows their sound and style.
- **Remix tracks:** what they play, made anew; **Strength** says how far from it.
- **Repaint a stretch of a take:** make part of a take anew and keep the rest.

## Phrases in Composer

In a clip's [Composer](../composer/#generate-a-phrase) window, **Generate** writes a piano phrase: a new one, a continuation of yours, or a harmony to your melody. Nothing changes your clip until you keep a take.

## Split a song into stems

Right-click an audio clip and choose **Separate stems**. The clip shows how far it has got, and its menu can cancel it. When it is done, a group named after the clip holds an audio track for each stem (vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and the rest), each in a colour of its own, and the original track is muted.

## Turn audio into notes

An audio clip's menu has **Convert Melody**, **Convert Harmony** and **Convert Drums to New MIDI Track**: Solmara listens to the clip and writes what it hears as a MIDI clip. Dropping an audio clip on a MIDI track does the same, heard on that track's instrument.

## A cover for your song

A project can have a cover, shown in the top bar and in your songs. Right-click it to make a new one from the song's music, with a few words of your own if you like.
