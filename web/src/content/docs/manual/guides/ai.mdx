Solmara's AI runs on your own computer, on its graphics card where it has one. Nothing you make is sent anywhere, and everything a model makes is an ordinary part of your project: a take is an audio clip, a phrase is notes, a stem is a track.

## The models

Each model downloads the first time you use it. **Preferences › AI** lists them with their description, source, licence and size: **Download model** fetches one, and **Check model** checks an installed one and repairs it. **Run on** chooses the processor or a graphics card.

## Generate music on a track

1. Add an **audio track**, right-click its row and choose **Create AI generation clip**. Drag it as long as the music should be; a clip made without dragging is 16 bars.
2. The clip's panel has three columns:
   - **Music:** describe the music in words, and choose the **voice**, **Instrumental** or **Vocals** (and in which language), and the **quality**: **Fast**, a take in about a minute, or **Best**, slower and more careful.
   - **Lyrics**, for a sung take: sections tagged `[Verse 1]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, each in a colour of its own.
   - **Takes:** every take you make, newest first.
3. Press **Generate**. Each press makes a new take; you can keep working and playing while it does.
4. Click a take to put it in the clip, and its star to keep it. <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> play the take above or below; <kbd>Enter</kbd> puts it in the clip.

### Make it fit your song

**From the project**, under the music, lets a take start from what you have:

- **Continue the music before it:** the take carries on from what the tracks you choose play before the clip.
- **Accompany tracks with a part:** a part made to fit what your tracks play: vocals, drums, bass, guitar, keys, strings and more.
- **Take tracks as a reference of style:** the take borrows their sound and style.
- **Remix tracks:** what they play, made anew; **Strength** says how far from it.
- **Repaint a stretch of a take:** make part of a take anew and keep the rest.

## Phrases in Composer

In a clip's [Composer](../composer/#generate-a-phrase) window, **Generate** writes a piano phrase: a new one, a continuation of yours, or a harmony to your melody. Nothing changes your clip until you keep a take.

## A drummer: the Drum Player

The **Drum Player** writes drum parts: beats and fills with the timing and dynamics of a real drummer. It is a model of Solmara's own, it comes with the app, and it runs on the processor, so there is nothing to download.

1. Make a MIDI clip on a drum track, 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars long, and open its Composer window. On a drum track, **Generate** starts on **Drums**; elsewhere, choose **Drums** in place of **Piano** at the left of its strip.
2. Say what to play. Choose a **genre** (any, or one of 22, from rock, funk and jazz to Afro-Cuban and New Orleans) and the **part**, **Any**, **Beat** or **Fill**. Or type keywords, such as `funk, fill, no ride`.
3. Press **Generate**. It writes a batch of takes into the list at the right. The chosen take shows in the clip's lanes, one lane for each kit piece.
4. Click a take to show it, and press <kbd>Space</kbd> to hear it: it plays in the clip, round its loop, on the track's own drums. Double-click it, press **Keep in this clip** or <kbd>Enter</kbd> to keep it: its notes go into the clip as ordinary notes, one step to undo. **As new clip** puts it in a clip after this one instead.

The tempo, the time signature and the length come from your song and the clip. The Drum Player plays in 4/4, at a steady tempo from 20 to 300 BPM.

### Shape it

The **···** menu has more:

- **Match a performance:** how **complex**, how **intense** and how **varied** the takes should be. It writes many ideas and keeps the ones closest to what you ask.
- **Kit:** for each kind of kit piece (kick, snare, hi-hat, ride, crash, toms and the rest), **Allow** it, **Require** it at least once, or **Leave out** it, for a beat on the ride with no hi-hat.
- **Opening:** a **New idea**, or keep the clip's first bar, two bars or four, and let it carry on from what you wrote.
- **Seed:** the same seed writes the same takes again, so a good batch can be found again.
- **Runs on:** the processor, or a graphics card for larger batches faster.

## Split a song into stems

Right-click an audio clip and choose **Separate stems**. The clip shows how far it has got, and its menu can cancel it. When it is done, a group named after the clip holds an audio track for each stem (vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and the rest), each in a colour of its own, and the original track is muted.

## Turn audio into notes

An audio clip's menu has **Convert Melody to New MIDI Track**, **Convert Harmony to New MIDI Track** and **Convert Drums to New MIDI Track**: Solmara listens to the clip and writes what it hears as a MIDI clip. Dropping an audio clip on a MIDI track does the same, heard on that track's instrument.

## A cover for your song

A project can have a cover, shown in the top bar and in your songs. Click the cover, or the mark at the top bar's left where there is none yet, and choose **Generate a cover…** (the first time, **Get the cover art model…**): it writes a scene from the song's music, which you can change or replace with words of your own, and makes takes to choose from; **Use as cover** keeps one. **Choose an image…** uses a picture of your own instead.
