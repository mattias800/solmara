title: The library and sounds
description: Find instruments, effects, presets and samples, keep favourites, save your own, and get more sounds.
---

The **library** at the left holds everything you can add to a song.

## Find your way in it

Down its left edge:

- **Collections:** **Favourites**, **Recent**, and your own collections, each with a colour.
- **Categories:** **Sounds** (the instruments' presets by kind: bass, pad, lead…), **Drums**, **Instruments** by family, **Audio effects** by group, **Tones** (guitar tones you downloaded), **MIDI effects**, **Plugins** and **Tracks** (track and group presets).
- **Places:** the **Factory** library that comes with Solmara, your **User library**, and **Content** you downloaded.

The list beside them shows what is in the one you chose, in folders. A device's presets are under it, in their categories.

**Search** looks through everything at once: devices, presets and plugins. The foot of the library says what the thing under the mouse is, its description and tags; click a tag to search for it.

Once you have clicked a row, the arrow keys move through the list, as in a file browser: <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> choose, <kbd>Right</kbd> opens a folder and <kbd>Left</kbd> closes it.

## Add from it

- **Double-click** an instrument, effect or preset to add it to the track you are on, or a track preset to add the track.
- **Drag** it onto a track, into a device chain, or below the tracks for a new track.
- **Click** a device to see it in the inspector: what it is, what it is for, its presets and its controls.

## Keep what you like

- **Right-click** anything to add it to **Favourites** or to a collection of your own.
- **Save a device as a preset:** right-click its title in the chain. **Save a track:** right-click its header. A preset keeps a name, a category, tags, a description and an author.
- **Your own folders**, such as a shared drive, are added in **Preferences › Library**.

## Bring presets from elsewhere

- **File › Import Vital and Serum presets as Vintergatan presets…** turns a folder of them into Vintergatan presets, wavetables and all.
- **File › Import SysEx voices or programs as presets…** reads the banks of the synths Solmara can open: DX7 voice banks for Six-Op FM, programs for Norrsken, patches and performances for the Jet-8000.
- **Sample libraries** in SFZ, DecentSampler and SoundFont formats play in the **Sampler**.

## Get more sounds

**View › Get content…** lists free sound packs from their makers: drum kits, sample libraries, guitar tones and synth banks. Each shows its terms before you take it. **View › Downloads…** shows what is downloading. Guitar tones from TONE3000 are found and downloaded right there too, after signing in.
