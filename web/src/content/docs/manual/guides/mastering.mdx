Mastering is the last step: the finished mix made to sound its best and as loud as it should be, wherever it is played. Solmara has a family of mastering devices made to work together, and a **suite** that shows them all at once.

## Add a mastering chain

- **The master's mixer strip** has a mastering slot at its head. Click it to add the default chain, **Streaming Master**, or right-click it for another, to open the suite, or to switch mastering on and off.
- **The key beside the LUFS cell** in the top bar does the same.
- **The library's Mastering category** has four chains, from gentle to club-loud: **Gentle**, **Streaming**, **Loud** and **Club Master**. Drop one in the master's chain.

A chain is these devices, in this order, each also an effect for any track:

| Device | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Morgonrodnad EQ** | An eight-band parametric EQ, each band on both sides, the middle or the sides, dynamic if you like, and linear phase |
| **Plejaderna Multiband** | Four bands, each held down or lifted on its own |
| **Trollspegeln Imager** | Width by band, the lows folded to mono |
| **Polarnatt Clipper** | Takes the fastest peaks off before the limiter, in six curves from hard to valve |
| **Himlavalv Limiter** | A true-peak limiter: loud and clean |

**Solvind EQ**, an equalizer that listens, turning down what masks and up what hides, is in the family too, and **Tidvatten Compressor**, an upward and downward compressor in three bands, goes well with it.

## The suite

**Suite**, over the master's chain, opens the mastering suite: every device of the master's chain in full, a column each, side by side, with the **Analyzer** as the last column. A knob turned here is the knob in the chain.

<Shot src={suite} alt="The mastering suite: the chain's row along the top with Before, After and Mastering, a column for each of the five devices, the Analyzer at the right, and the song's loudness along the foot." />

- **The chain's row** along the top: drag to reorder, a lamp to bypass a device, **+** to add one.
- **Mastering on and off** takes the whole chain out, and its latency with it, for recording and playing in; on puts it back.
- **Before and After**, matched in loudness: Before plays the mix as it came in, turned to the loudness the master leaves with, so you hear what the chain does and not just that it is louder.
- **Along the foot**, the song's loudness section by section. Click a section to play from there.

## The Analyzer

**Karlavagnen Analyzer** measures the mix as streaming services do: integrated and short-term loudness in **LUFS**, the **true peak**, the spectrum and the stereo field. It lets you hear the song at a service's level, as a listener will. Click the **LUFS** cell in the top bar to open it. The cell shows the song's loudness while it plays, its lamp amber over -1 dB true peak and red over 0.

## How loud?

Streaming services turn loud songs down, so there is little to gain past their level. Around **-14 LUFS** with true peaks under **-1 dB** suits most of them; **Streaming Master** aims there. **Loud** and **Club Master** are for when you want it hotter.

When it sounds right, [render the song](../projects/#render-your-song).
