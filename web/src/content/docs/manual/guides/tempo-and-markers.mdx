---
title: Tempo, time and markers
description: Set and change the tempo, time signatures, the loop, markers and moving around the song.
---

## Tempo

**Tempo** in the top bar is the tempo where the playhead is: drag it, or double-click it and type. **Tap** beside it takes the tempo from your taps.

A song can change tempo. Open the **tempo map** from the tempo's menu to add a tempo change at the playhead, set its bar and tempo, and tick **Ramp** to glide into it from the tempo before. The ruler marks each tempo change along its bottom edge, and playback, the loop and every delay that keeps time follow the map.

## Time signatures

The tempo map adds time signatures too, by bar: 3/4 for a waltz, 7/8 for a turn, back to 4/4. The ruler numbers the bars by them, and the grid starts again at each bar, so the first beat of a bar is always a line.

You can also right-click the ruler to add a tempo change or a time signature where you clicked.

## The playhead and the loop

- **Click the ruler** to move the playhead there, even while the song plays. A click on an empty place of a track puts it on the nearest grid line.
- **Play** starts where you last put the playhead, and **Stop** returns it there, so you can play the same passage again and again.
- **Loop** in the top bar repeats a range. Set the range in the ruler by <kbd>Shift</kbd>-dragging it, or drag either end of it.
- **Follow playhead**, in the arrangement's title or the View menu, scrolls the view along with the song.

Stopping lets reverb and delay tails ring out in their own time.

## Markers

Markers name places in a song: *Intro*, *Verse*, *Drop*.

- **Show the marker lane** with **Markers** over the track headers, or **View › Marker lane**. It sits under the ruler.
- **Add a marker:** double-click an empty place in the lane (with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd> to place it off the grid), or right-click the ruler or the Markers key to add one at the playhead.
- **Jump to one:** click its flag. <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> jump to the previous and next marker.
- **Right-click a flag** to rename it, move it to the playhead, or delete it.

## The metronome

**Click** in the top bar turns the metronome on. Its menu sets the sound (a click, a beep, wood block, hi-hat, cowbell, rim shot or clave), its level, and the count-in before recording. It clicks on the beats of the time signature, the first of each bar accented.

## Zoom and scroll

- **Zoom** with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd> and the scroll wheel (or pinch on a touchpad), around the mouse; or the **−** and **+** at the right of the arrangement's title, where clicking the percentage goes back to 100 %.
- **Scroll** up and down with the wheel, and sideways with <kbd>Shift</kbd> and the wheel, or a sideways swipe.
