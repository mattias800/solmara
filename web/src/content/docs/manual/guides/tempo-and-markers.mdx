---
title: Tempo, time and markers
description: Set and change the tempo, time signatures, the loop, markers and moving around the song.
---

## Tempo

**TEMPO** in the top bar is the tempo where the playhead is: drag it, or double-click it and type. **TAP**, at its right, takes the tempo from your taps: from the fourth tap, the tempo follows them.

A song can change tempo. Click the **TEMPO** label, with the arrow after it, to open the **tempo map**: **Add tempo at playhead** adds a change, its row sets where it is and its BPM, and **RAMP** glides into it from the tempo before, **BEND** curving the glide early or late. Playback, the loop and every delay that keeps time follow the map.

To see the tempo as a line over the song, right-click the corner above the track headers and choose **Tempo lane**.

## Time signatures

The tempo map adds time signatures too, with **Add time signature at playhead bar**: 3/4 for a waltz, 7/8 for a turn, back to 4/4. The ruler numbers the bars by them, and the grid starts again at each bar, so the first beat of a bar is always a line.

You can also right-click the ruler: **Add a tempo change here**, or **Add a time signature at bar** the one you clicked in.

## The playhead and the loop

- **Click the ruler** to move the playhead there, even while the song plays. A click on an empty place of a track puts it on the nearest grid line.
- **Play** starts where you last put the playhead, and **Stop** (<kbd>Space</kbd>) returns it there, so you can play the same passage again and again.
- **Loop** in the top bar plays a range round and round. Drag in the band along the top of the ruler to choose the range, or type its ends beside the tempo. <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd> loops the selected clips.
- **Follow playhead**, a key in the arrangement's title, turns the page as the playhead leaves the view. Scrolling by hand pauses it until you play again.

Stopping lets reverb and delay tails ring out in their own time.

## Markers

Markers name places in a song: *Intro*, *Verse*, *Drop*.

- **Show the marker lane:** click the arrow after **TRACKS**, above the track headers, and choose **Markers**. It sits under the ruler.
- **Add a marker:** double-click an empty place in the lane (with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd> to place it off the grid), right-click the lane or the ruler and choose **Add marker here**, or use **Go to marker › Add at playhead** in the TRACKS menu.
- **Jump to one:** click its flag, or choose it under **Go to marker**. <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> jump to the previous and next marker.
- **Right-click a flag** to edit it, move it to the playhead, or delete it.

## The metronome

The **metronome** key in the top bar turns it on. Right-click it for its sound (Click, Beep, Wood block, Hi-hat, Cowbell, Rim shot or Clave), its level, and the **count-in before recording**: none, or one, two or four bars. It clicks on the beats of the time signature, the first of each bar accented.

## Zoom and scroll

- **Zoom** with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd> and the scroll wheel (or pinch on a touchpad), around the mouse; with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>+</kbd>; or with the **−** and **+** at the right of the arrangement's title, where clicking the percentage goes back to 100 % (<kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd>).
- **Scroll** up and down with the wheel, and sideways with <kbd>Shift</kbd> and the wheel, or a sideways swipe.
