title: Preferences
description: What each tab of the Preferences window sets.
---

**File › Preferences…** (<kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>) holds what belongs to your computer rather than to a project. A project keeps its own settings; these stay with you from song to song.

## Audio

- **Audio output:** the device Solmara plays through, or the system's default, which it follows when that changes. The size of block asked of the device sets how quickly it responds against how hard the computer works.
- **Take it for Solmara alone** (Windows): plays past the system's mixer, in the smallest blocks your device allows, for the lowest latency. Nothing else is heard through the device meanwhile.
- **Render on:** how many processor cores render tracks beside the audio. The sound is the same with any number; it takes effect the next time Solmara starts.
- **Audio input:** the device you record from, and the **offset** a recording is moved by, if your recordings sit a little late.
- What the stream is doing: its device, rate and latency, how many blocks it has played and dropped, the level now, and a **Test tone**. If the system's own volume mixer has turned Solmara down, it says so and offers to put it back.

## MIDI

**MIDI inputs:** which keyboards play, every one or those you tick, and the last message that came in. A keyboard plays the armed tracks, or the selected MIDI track. MIDI input is on Windows and macOS for now.

## Plugins

The CLAP, VST3 and Audio Unit plugins installed, each with its kind, format and maker, and the folders they are looked for in. Files that could not be read are listed with why; **Read again** tries one, **Read all again** looks anew. Plugins are read beside the editor, so Solmara opens at once and a plugin that crashes as it loads is left out and named. Here too: whether <kbd>Space</kbd> in a plugin's own window plays and stops the song.

## Library

The folders presets are found in. Add your own, such as a shared drive. **Preview** sets whether presets play a few notes as you browse them.

## Look

The palette Solmara is drawn in: **Console**, **Midnight**, which is darker, or **Control Room**, warm with an amber accent. A click shows it at once.

## Recovery

How often your work is saved in the background, so it can be brought back after a crash: from every 5 seconds to every hour, or never.

## AI

The AI models Solmara runs on your computer: each with its description, source, licence and size. **Download model** fetches one; **Check model** checks an installed one and repairs it. **Run on** chooses the processor or a graphics card for them.

## Names

Your own names for Solmara's devices, shown in place of the built-in names wherever a device appears. They belong to you, not to a project. **Import…** and **Export…** move them between computers or share a set.
