---
title: Preferences
description: What each page of the Preferences window sets.
---

**File › Preferences…** (<kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>) holds what belongs to your computer rather than to a project. A project keeps its own settings; these stay with you from song to song. The pages are down the left in three groups, **Sound**, **Content** and **Workspace**. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the window.

## Sound

### Audio

- **The stream's state**, at the top: what Solmara is playing through, or why no sound is going out, with its driver, rate, block size, latency and dropouts, the level now, and a **Test tone**. If the system's own volume mixer has turned Solmara down, it says so and offers to put it back.
- **Output:** the **output device**, or the system's default, which it follows when that changes; the **sample rate**, the interface's own unless you choose one; and the **block size**, how quickly it responds against how hard the computer works. **Exclusive mode** (Windows) plays past the system's mixer, in the smallest blocks your device allows, for the lowest latency. Nothing else is heard through the device meanwhile.
- **Input and recording:** the **input device** you record from, and the **recording offset** a recording is moved earlier by, if your recordings land a little late.
- **Performance:** how many **render threads** render tracks beside the audio (the sound is the same with any number; it takes effect the next time Solmara starts), and whether Vintergatan plays in **Eco** while you work, about a third of the cost. Renders and exports always play High.

### MIDI

Which keyboards play: **every input**, or **only these**, each switched on or off. The foot shows the last message that came in. A keyboard plays the armed tracks, or the selected MIDI track. MIDI input is on Windows and macOS for now.

### Plugins

The CLAP, VST3 and Audio Unit plugins installed, found by name or maker and by kind, each with its maker, kind, format and version. Files that could not be read come first, with why; **Read again** tries one, **Read them again** all of them, and **Read all again** looks anew. Plugins are read beside the editor, so Solmara opens at once and a plugin that crashes as it loads is left out and named. Under **Behavior**: whether a plugin in several formats is listed once, the **preferred format**, and whether <kbd>Space</kbd> in a plugin's own window plays and stops the song. Last, the folders plugins are looked for in.

## Content

### Library

- **Presets:** the folders presets are found in. **Add folder…** adds your own, such as a shared drive.
- **Samples:** your own folders of samples, anywhere on a disk, each a place of its own in the library.
- **Sample libraries:** the folders sample libraries are kept in, and the one **Get content** installs new ones into.
- **Browsing and previews:** whether imported loops follow the tempo, whether presets play a few notes as you browse them, and whether a click in the library plays what it is on.

### AI models

Where the models run (**Run models on**: automatically, the processor, or a graphics card), and what the computer has for them. Then a card for each kind of model, with its description, license, size and state: **song generation**, with **Download model**, **Check model**, which checks an installed one and repairs it, and a short test; **stem separation**; **cover art**, with the size covers are made at; and the **MIDI models**. Last, the models on your disk, each with **Remove**.

## Workspace

### Appearance

The palette Solmara is drawn in, each shown as a little editor in its colors: **Console**, **Midnight**, which is darker, or **Control Room**, warm with an amber accent. A click shows it at once. **Reduce motion** makes what would glide jump.

### Device names

Your own names for Solmara's devices, shown in place of the built-in names wherever a device appears. They belong to you, not to a project. Right click a device to name it; **Import…** and **Export…** move a set between computers or share one.

### Saving & recovery

How often a recovery copy of your unsaved work is kept, so it can be brought back after a crash: from every 5 seconds to every 10 minutes, or never. **Find recovery copies…** looks for them now.

### About

Shows the first-start tour or the welcome again at the next start, the commands scripts and agents set these preferences with, and the credits: the sounds, fonts, code and models that come with Solmara and the people who made them, with their licenses.
