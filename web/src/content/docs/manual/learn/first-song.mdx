In this walk-through you make a short loop: a drum beat, a bassline under it, chords over it, a reverb to put them in a room, and a mix you render to a file. It takes about twenty minutes, and every step can be undone with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>.

<Aside type="tip">Rest the mouse on anything you are unsure of: the help line at the bottom left says what it is and what clicking or dragging it does.</Aside>

## 1. A beat

<Steps>

1. Choose **File › New project**. You get an empty song with one MIDI track.

2. In the library at the left, open **Instruments › Drums** and drag **PH-8**, a drum machine, onto the track's header. Double-click the track's name and call it *Drums*.

3. Right-click an empty place on the Drums row, in the first bar, and choose **Create Drum pattern**. A one-bar pattern appears.

4. Select the pattern and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open it. Each row is one of the PH-8's seventeen drums, each square a step of a sixteenth note. Click to strike:
   - **Bass drum:** steps 1, 5, 9 and 13.
   - **Snare drum:** steps 5 and 13.
   - **Closed hat:** every other step: 1, 3, 5 and on.

5. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to play, and **Loop** in the top bar so it goes round. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to go back to the arrangement.

</Steps>

Want more feel? Drag a hit up or down for its loudness, or try **Swing** in the pattern's title row. [Drum and step patterns](../../guides/drum-patterns/) has the rest.

## 2. A bassline

<Steps>

1. Press <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> for a new MIDI track, and call it *Bass*.

2. Drag **Mini Moonbeam** from **Instruments › Synths** onto it. Click its name in its title, in the devices panel, to browse its presets, and pick one from **Bass**.

3. On the Bass row, **double-click and drag** across four bars to draw a clip that long. Select it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open the piano roll.

4. **Double-click and drag** to draw notes, one bar each, low on the keyboard: **A**, then **F**, then **C**, then **G**. Click the keys at the left first to hear where they are.

5. Play. For a busier line, cut each long note into eighths, or draw shorter notes on the off-beats.

</Steps>

## 3. Chords

<Steps>

1. Make one more MIDI track, *Keys*, and drag **Juniper Eight** onto it. Pick a preset from **Pad**.

2. Draw a four-bar clip on its row, as before, then **double-click** it. It opens in **Composer**.

3. Turn on **Chords** in Composer's bar. **Double-click** the chord lane above each bar to put a chord there. Then click a chord and pick from **Fits** at the right: try **Am**, **F**, **C** and **G**, so the chords sit on your bassline. Click a suggestion to hear it, double-click to keep it.

4. Along the foot, choose a sequence: **Held and strummed** for long chords, or **Broken and arpeggios** to play them as an arpeggio. Give it to **All chords**.

5. Press **Clip** to hear the chords alone, then play the song.

</Steps>

[Compose with chords](../../guides/composer/) shows what else Composer can do: voicing, layers, and writing a phrase with AI.

## 4. A room for it

<Steps>

1. Press <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> for a return track, and call it *Space*.

2. Drag **Gemini Plate**, a plate reverb, from **Audio effects › Delay and reverb** onto it.

3. Show the mixer with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd>. On the Keys strip, turn its send to Space up. The chords now ring in the plate.

</Steps>

## 5. Mix and finish

<Steps>

1. In the mixer, set the faders so the kick and the bass sit together and the chords sit behind them. Keep the master meter out of the red.

2. To make the loop longer, select the three clips (<kbd>Shift</kbd>-click them) and press <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> to duplicate them after themselves.

3. Save with <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>.

4. **File › Render project…** renders the song to a WAV or FLAC file.

</Steps>

You have a song. From here:

- [Work with clips](../../guides/clips/) to build it into verses and choruses.
- [Automate a sound](../../guides/automation/) so a filter opens into the drop.
- [Record](../../guides/recording/) your own playing.
