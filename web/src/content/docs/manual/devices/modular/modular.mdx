**A design of Solmara's own.**

A modular synth that plays the track's notes: patch sample-rate oscillators, filters, envelopes, sequencers, utilities, effects and ordinary devices with polyphonic voltage cables.

[Modular on the website](/solmara/modular/)

## Appendix: the factory presets

Modular comes with 65 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass:** Acid Line, Folded Sine Bass, Key-Tracked Wobble Bass, Ladder Bass, Reese Bass, Sub Pulse Bass, Through-Zero FM Bass.
- **Bell:** Glass Bell, Struck Bell.
- **Drone:** Cross-FM Storm, Drifting Drone, Feedback Loop Drone, Filter Cross-FM, Oscillator Wall, Slow FM Drone.
- **Drums:** Burst Clap, Gate-Bent Kick, Noise Snare, Ringing Filter Kick, Sine Kick, Six-Square Hats, Tuned Tom.
- **Generative:** Airport Loops, Arabesque Canon, Coin-Toss Hats, Euclidean Timelines, Krell Drift, Logistic Chaos Melody, Looping Random Melody, Phase Drift, Probability Hats, Random Noise Bounce, Rungler Chaos, Stumbling Random, West Coast Noodle.
- **Keys:** Electric Tines, First Patch, Modal Keys.
- **Lead:** Breathy Flute, Folded Voice, Pulse Width Lead, Ring Bell Lead, Sync Sweep Lead, Talking Lead, Waveguide Reed.
- **Pad:** Chorus Saw Pad, Grain Shimmer Pad, Vowel Table Pad.
- **Percussion:** Bouncing Ball, FM Ping Bongo, Timpani, Two-Tone Cowbell, West Coast Bongo.
- **Pluck:** Folded Harp, Karplus-Strong Pluck, Pinged Marimba, Resonant Pluck.
- **Sequence:** Berlin School, Clockwork Steps, Rack Beat, Ratchet Run, Sample and Hold Bleeps.
- **Strings:** Bowed Resonator, Bowed Saw Strings, Phaser Strings.
