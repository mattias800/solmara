**A design of Solmara's own.**

A tone captured with Neural Amp Modeler, an amp, a pedal or a whole rig, played from its .nam file (NAM's A2, Lite and Full, and A1's WaveNets and LSTMs), which is kept with the project; with an input gain, a calibration to the level the tone was captured at, the output leveled by its loudness, and an output gain. It holds the audio back by 64 frames, and 128 where the model's rate is not the project's.

[A2 Modeler Amp on the website](/solmara/guitar/#a2-modeller-amp)

Each of the device's controls by the name automation and the inspector give it, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.
