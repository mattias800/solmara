**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the late-1960s American twin-speaker clean combo with reverb and vibrato.

A modeled American clean guitar combo of two channels, without its speakers or reverb: in each channel an input valve that drives a passive treble, middle, and bass stack straight from its plate, a volume with a bright switch, and a second valve; the vibrato channel has a third after a large divider, more gain, and an optical tremolo. A long-tailed phase inverter and four beam tetrodes in fixed bias with a strong feedback loop give a big, clean power stage that breaks up late, on two speakers' load. Volume, treble, middle, and bass, bright, the channel and the high or low input, the tremolo with its speed and intensity, and a quality switch for how far above the sample rate the circuit is solved. It wants a cabinet after it, the speaker cabinet device. Modeled on the 100-watt two-speaker combo with the black panel of the mid-1960s, the clean sound of country, soul, and session guitar, built from its published drawing.
