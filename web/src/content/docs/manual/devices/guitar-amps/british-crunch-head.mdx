**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the early-1980s British master-volume valve head of hard rock.

A modeled British master-volume guitar head of one channel, without a cabinet: an input valve, a cold-biased second stage that clips one side of the wave first, a third stage into a cathode follower, a passive tone stack whose knobs lean on each other, a master before the phase inverter, and four European output pentodes in push-pull on a speaker's load, with the presence in the power stage's feedback loop. Preamp, bass, middle, treble, presence, and master, a high input and a low one that passes the input valve by, and a quality switch for how far above the sample rate the circuit is solved. It wants a cabinet after it, the speaker cabinet device. Modeled on the British 100-watt master-volume lead head of the early 1980s, the crunch and lead sound of that decade's hard rock.
