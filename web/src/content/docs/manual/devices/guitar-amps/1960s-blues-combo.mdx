**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the mid-1960s British 2x12 valve combo of blues-rock.

A modeled British guitar combo of the mid-1960s, without its speakers: the jumpered head's circuit of two channels and four input jacks, with its own numbers, two beam tetrodes in push-pull, no capacitor across the first channel's volume, and a tremolo on the second channel. Two volumes, bass, middle, treble, presence, the tremolo's intensity and speed, which jack the guitar is in and whether a jumper joins the channels, and a quality switch. It wants a cabinet after it, the speaker cabinet device. Modeled on the British 2x12 combo of the mid-1960s that blues-rock broke up through.
