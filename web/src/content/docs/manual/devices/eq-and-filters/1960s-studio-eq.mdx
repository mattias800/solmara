**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the in-house seven-band stepped graphic equalizer of a 1960s Detroit record label.

A modeled seven-band stepped graphic equalizer in the style of the in-house equalizers of a 1960s American record label's studios: passive coils and capacitors in two interleaved constant-resistance networks (50 Hz to 12.5 kHz, -8 to +8 dB in whole steps), so a band's width follows its gain and two bands of a network pull on each other; a make-up amplifier that clips hard at its rails, and the transformers, the input's core giving low notes a third harmonic. At four times the sample rate.
