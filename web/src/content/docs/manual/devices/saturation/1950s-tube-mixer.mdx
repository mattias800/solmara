**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the 1950s American valve broadcast microphone mixer.

A modeled valve microphone mixer of the 1950s American broadcast and studio amplifiers, as a saturator: a line input, or a microphone input through its transformer, the channel's gain into a high-mu preamplifier valve, the mixer's simple bass and treble, the master level into a single-ended output valve on its output transformer, which rounds and then flattens the top of the wave, a mix, and the unit's own hiss. In stereo, each channel through its own valves, at four times the sample rate.
