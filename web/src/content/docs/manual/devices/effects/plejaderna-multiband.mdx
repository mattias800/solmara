**A design of Solmara's own.**

A multiband compressor for the master and for buses, the mastering family's: four bands, split where the crossover splits and summing back flat, each a compressor over its threshold, by its ratio, or under 1:1 one that lifts it, with its own attack, release, gain, solo and mute. A new one changes nothing until a band is set to.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Plejaderna Multiband. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bus

- **Drum Bus Punch**: The kick's band held with a slow attack so its front stays and its body is tight, the snare's band lifted a little where it cracks.
- **Parallel Squash**: Every band crushed at 6 to 1, fast, and half of it under what came in: the quiet details come up while the hits keep their shape.
- **Vocal Even**: A vocal held even through its body, its sibilance band quick and firm so the S sounds do not jump out.

### Mastering

- **Air Lift**: The top band lifted where it already sings, an upward expander at 0.7 to 1: air that rises with the music rather than a fixed shelf.
- **De-Harsh Top**: Only the top and the upper middle turned down when they get sharp, quickly, and let go as quickly: cymbals and a bright voice softened only when they bite.
- **Gentle Master Glue**: A light hold on every band of a master, a dB or two each at 1.5 to 1 with slow times, so the parts move together without anyone hearing a compressor.
- **Loud Master Bands**: Every band held harder, at 2.5 to 1, the lows slow and the highs quick, with a little gain back: a dense, even master before the limiter.
- **Tame the Lows**: The low end held steady under the rest: the bottom band at 3 to 1 with a slow attack so a kick's front gets through, the others left alone.
