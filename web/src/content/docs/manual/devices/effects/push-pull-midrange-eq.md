**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1950s American passive valve mid-range equaliser.

A modelled passive mid-range equaliser with a valve make-up amplifier: a low peak from 200 Hz to 1 kHz, a dip from 200 Hz to 7 kHz, and a high peak from 1.5 to 5 kHz, each a coil and a capacitor on a switch, in one network whose sections lean on each other. A switch takes the valves and the transformers out for a clean amplifier at a fraction of the processing. Modelled on the passive valve mid-range equaliser of the late 1950s, the program equaliser's companion, for the body of a voice or a guitar, a dip for the boxiness of a drum, and presence.
