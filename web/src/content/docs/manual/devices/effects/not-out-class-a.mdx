**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1970s British class-A console preamp and equaliser.

A modelled console channel of the class-A kind, for shaping single tracks: a line transformer, a stepped gain switch, an amplifier of three transistors, an equaliser of switched frequencies, and an output amplifier whose last transistor drives a gapped output transformer. A shelf at 12 kHz, a presence band at 360 Hz to 7.2 kHz on coils, a low shelf at 35, 60, 110, or 220 Hz, and a high-pass of 18 dB an octave at 50, 80, 160, or 300 Hz, with switches for the equaliser and the phase and a trim. Turned up, the output stage runs out of current and its transformer fills on low notes, so the lows thicken and distort first, mostly in the second and third harmonics. In stereo, each channel through its own circuits, with a quality switch for how far above the sample rate they are solved. Modelled on the British microphone and line amplifier with equaliser of the early 1970s, built on discrete class-A amplifier cards between transformers.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Not Out Class A. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### EQ

- **Acoustic Sparkle**: The high-pass at 80 Hz, the low shelf down at 220 Hz to thin the body's boom, a little taken out at 700 Hz, and the high shelf well up for the strings.
- **Bass DI Growl**: A bass straight from its instrument, through the channel with the gain at +15: the output stage runs short of current on the low notes and adds a second and a third harmonic that are heard on a small speaker. The low shelf up at 110 Hz, the presence band up at 700 Hz for the growl, the highs a little down, and the trim back by 15 dB.
- **Electric Bite**: The high-pass at 80 Hz, the presence band up at 1.6 kHz where a guitar cuts through a mix, the lows a little down at 110 Hz, and the gain at +5 with the trim back by as much.
- **Kick Weight**: The low shelf lifted at 60 Hz and the presence band cut at 360 Hz, which takes the box out of a kick drum and leaves its weight, with the high shelf up a little for the beater. The gain at +5, so the output stage's lows thicken a loud kick, and the trim back by as much.
- **Snare Crack**: The low shelf up at 220 Hz for the drum's body, the presence band up at 4.8 kHz for its crack, the high shelf a little up, and the high-pass at 80 Hz to keep the kick's spill out.
- **Vocal Presence**: The high-pass at 80 Hz, the low shelf a little down at 110 Hz against the proximity effect, the presence band up at 3.2 kHz, where a voice's words are, and the high shelf up for air.

### Saturation

- **Drive Only**: The equaliser out and the gain at +20, with the trim back by 20 dB: nothing but the amplifiers and the transformers, driven so that the loud parts of a track come up against the output stage and its lows thicken. Turn the trim to taste.
- **Gentle Glue**: The gain at +10 with the trim back by as much and the equaliser in with a touch of air and a little low shelf at 35 Hz: the transformers' colour on a whole mix, with a loud low note given a little more of it.
