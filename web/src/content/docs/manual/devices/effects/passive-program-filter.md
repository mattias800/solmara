A modelled passive high- and low-cut filter with no amplifier: a low cut from 50 Hz to 2 kHz and a high cut from 1.5 to 15 kHz, each a coil and capacitor section on a stepped switch, the two loading each other, and a key to throw it in and out. Modelled on the American program filters of the 1950s and 1960s, for rumble and hiss, a thinned or telephone-like voice, or a dark, distant effect.
