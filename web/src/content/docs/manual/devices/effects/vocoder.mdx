**A design of Solmara's own.**

Makes a carrier speak through 4 to 40 bands of an input's envelopes. Noise, external audio, the input itself, or a pitch-following oscillator; with formants, freeze, and a band patchbay.

## Presets

11 factory presets come with Vocoder. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Choir Pad**: Forty bands rise gently and linger for three seconds. Choose a sustained synth track as the external carrier.
- **Formant Down**: The voice is its own carrier, rebuilt with the synthesis bands seven semitones lower.
- **Formant Up**: The voice is its own carrier, rebuilt with the synthesis bands seven semitones higher.
- **Gated Vowels**: A noise carrier holds each vowel for an eighth note, following the project's tempo map.
- **Robot Voice**: Sixteen bands for a classic talking synth. Choose a synth track as the external carrier; until a source is chosen, the vocoder is silent.
- **Scrambled Speech**: The voice drives a fixed scrambled patch of sixteen synthesis bands, turning familiar words into a texture.
- **Singing Saw**: A saw follows one clear voice between 80 and 1000 Hz, keeping its last note through pauses.
- **Telephone Talker**: Eight bands from 300 Hz to 3.4 kHz remake the voice with a narrow, telephone-like range.
- **Tuned Robot**: A pitch-tracked pulse snaps to the project's scale before its glide: the voice sings its notes.
- **Whisper**: Dense noise follows the voice in twenty-four bands: a pitched voice becomes a whisper, with a little of its own highs keeping the consonants clear.
- **Wide Choir**: A synth carrier lingers for a second, with alternating bands hard left and right. Choose its external source track before playing.
