**A design of Solmara's own.**

A volume curve drawn over a stretch of the song, from a sixteenth to two bars, that turns a track down and up again in time: the pumping of a sidechained compressor under a kick, with no sidechain, or gates and swells. A second curve can move a filter's cutoff in the same time. Each cycle starts on the song's grid or with each note; ready shapes start it, and its curves are drawn.

## Presets

9 factory presets come with Pulsar Shaper. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bar Swell**: Up and down again over a bar: slow movement for a pad.
- **Eighth Gate**: Open for one sixteenth, shut for the next: a held pad chopped into a pulse.
- **Eighth Pump**: A short duck every eighth note, not quite all the way down: a bass or a chord that bounces.
- **Filter Pump**: The duck on the volume, and a low pass that opens with each beat and closes after it: pumping and breathing.
- **Long Duck**: A deeper, slower breath on each beat, the sound coming back only just before the next: the house pump.
- **Note Duck**: The duck starts with each note the track plays rather than on the grid, as a kick's own sidechain would.
- **Offbeat Pump**: The duck moved half a beat on, so the sound comes up on the beat and dips on the offbeat.
- **Quarter Duck**: The duck a sidechained compressor gives under a four-to-the-floor kick: down on each beat, held a moment, and back up quickly. On a pad, a bass or a whole bus.
- **Trance Gate**: Every sixteenth cut short, the trance gate on a supersaw.

## Controls

Each of the device's controls, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Depth | 0% to 100% | 100% |
| Length | 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 bar, 2 bars | 1/4 |
| Swing | 0% to 100% | 0% |
| Smooth | 0 ms to 20 ms | 2 ms |
| Offset | 0% to 100% | 0% |
| Restart on | Beat, Note | Beat |
| Moves | Volume, Filter, Both | Volume |
| Filter | Low pass, High pass | Low pass |
| Cutoff low | 20 Hz to 20000 Hz | 300 Hz |
| Cutoff high | 20 Hz to 20000 Hz | 8000 Hz |
| Resonance | 0% to 100% | 30% |
