**A design of Solmara's own.**

A modelled analogue filter: a transistor ladder, a squelchy diode ladder, a screaming Sallen-Key, a polished OTA cascade, or a smooth state variable, driven into saturation and ringing at high resonance, as low-, band-, or high-pass, with an LFO and an envelope follower to move its cutoff. The follower listens to the filter's input or another track by sidechain.

## Presets

9 factory presets come with Filter. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Acid Ladder**: A diode ladder that opens as the audio gets loud.
- **Auto-Wah**: A resonant band-pass that follows the playing.
- **Comb Shimmer**: A comb tuned to 440 Hz and its multiples, swept slowly an octave either way.
- **Dark Warmth**: A gentle ladder low-pass with a little drive.
- **Phaser Swirl**: The phaser's notches swept round 1 kHz, fed back for a deeper swirl.
- **Screaming Highpass**: A driven Sallen-Key high-pass on the edge.
- **Synced Wobble**: A transistor ladder swept on eighth notes.
- **Talking Vowels**: A formant filter on the vowel e, its formants raised by the playing.
- **Telephone**: A narrow band of the mids, as through a phone.
