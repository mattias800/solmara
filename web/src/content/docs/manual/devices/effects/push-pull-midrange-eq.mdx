**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1950s American passive valve mid-range equaliser.

A modelled passive mid-range equaliser with a valve make-up amplifier: a low peak from 200 Hz to 1 kHz, a dip from 200 Hz to 7 kHz, and a high peak from 1.5 to 5 kHz, each a coil and a capacitor on a switch, in one network whose sections lean on each other. A switch takes the valves and the transformers out for a clean amplifier at a fraction of the processing. Modelled on the passive valve mid-range equaliser of the late 1950s, the program equaliser's companion, for the body of a voice or a guitar, a dip for the boxiness of a drum, and presence.

## Presets

7 factory presets come with Push-Pull Midrange EQ. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass, Growl**: A bass's growl at 700 Hz, for a line that disappears under the mix.
- **Guitar, Forward**: Electric or acoustic guitar brought forward: a lift at 1 kHz and bite at 2 kHz.
- **Kick, Beater**: The kick's boxiness at 300 Hz down and its beater at 3 kHz forward.
- **Mix, Gentle Scoop**: A shallow dip at 500 Hz with a touch of presence: the middle of a busy mix cleared.
- **Snare, Crack**: The cardboard at 700 Hz out and the crack at 4 kHz up.
- **Vocal, Body and Presence**: A little chest at 200 Hz, the box at 500 Hz taken out, and presence at 3 kHz.
- **Vocal, Less Nasal**: A dip at 1 kHz for a voice that sounds pinched or nasal.
