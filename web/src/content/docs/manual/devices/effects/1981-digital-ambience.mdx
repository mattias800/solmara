**An emulation of a digital original,** modelled on the early-1980s British digital reverb of ambience, gated and reversed drums.

A digital reverb of the early 1980s, modelled as its algorithm and known for its programs: the short, dense ambience that puts a drum in a room, rooms, halls, and plates, the gated nonlinear program whose sound holds and then stops, a reverse that swells and stops, echoes, a chorus, and a freeze. A tapped early field and a recirculating loop at a clock of its own between steep converter filters. Pre-delay, decay with low and high multipliers, the converters' hiss, mix, and output. Edits never stop the tail.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with 1981 Digital Ambience. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bright Plate**: The first plate at 1.8 seconds with the highs ringing as long as the middle: a bright plate for vocals and snares.
- **Drum Ambience**: The ambience program at under a second: a dense burst of early reflections and a short quiet tail. Puts a dry kit in a room without washing it out.
- **Frozen Pad**: Freeze: what goes in hangs in the loop for as long as it plays. Under a pad or a drone.
- **Gated Kick**: Nonlin short and dark, at 150 ms with the highs cut: a kick that grows without a tail.
- **Gated Snare**: The gated Nonlin program at 300 ms: a wall of reflections that holds and then stops dead. The big snare of the 1980s.
- **Reverse Swell**: The reverse program at 400 ms: reflections that swell towards their end and stop, after a snare or a vocal word.
- **Tight Room**: The first room at under a second, its highs a little short: drums and guitars in a small live room.
- **Vocal Hall**: The second hall at 2.5 seconds, 40 ms of pre-delay, the bass a little long: a vocal's hall.
