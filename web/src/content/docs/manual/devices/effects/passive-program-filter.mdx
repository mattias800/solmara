**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1950s American passive high- and low-cut program filter.

A modelled passive high- and low-cut filter with no amplifier: a low cut from 50 Hz to 2 kHz and a high cut from 1.5 to 15 kHz, each a coil and capacitor section on a stepped switch, the two loading each other, and a key to throw it in and out. Modelled on the American program filters of the 1950s and 1960s, for rumble and hiss, a thinned or telephone-like voice, or a dark, distant effect.

## Presets

6 factory presets come with Passive Program Filter. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Dark and Distant**: A 5 kHz high cut: pushes a part back and darkens it.
- **Rumble and Hiss**: 50 Hz and 12 kHz: takes rumble and hiss away and leaves the program.
- **Small Radio**: 1 kHz to 4 kHz: a tinny little radio speaker.
- **Telephone**: 500 Hz to 3 kHz: a voice down a telephone line.
- **Thin Out**: A 250 Hz low cut: clears the low end out of a part that does not need it.
- **Vocal Cleanup**: 100 Hz and 15 kHz: takes handling noise and air hiss off a voice.
