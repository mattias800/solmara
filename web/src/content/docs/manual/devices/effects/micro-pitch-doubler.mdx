**An emulation of a digital original,** modelled on the micro-pitch doubling of the 1980s studio rack harmonisers.

A modelled studio micro-shift doubler, for lead and backing vocals, guitars, synths, and whole mixes: two pitch shifters a few cents up and a few cents down, each with a short delay of its own, one on each side, so that a sound is heard twice, a little apart in pitch and time, wide and still, without the wobble of a chorus. Three styles: I and II after the late-1980s American rack harmoniser's two micro-shift programs, I the grainier, III after the British rack pitch changer of the late 1970s, with longer delays and a harder splice. Detune and Delay scale the style's own shift and delays; Focus keeps the lows below it in the middle, unshifted.

## Controls

Each of the device's controls, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Detune | 50% to 200% | 100% |
| Delay | 50% to 200% | 100% |
| Focus | 20 Hz to 10000 Hz | 20 Hz |
