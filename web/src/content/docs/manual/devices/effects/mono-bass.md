Makes everything under a frequency mono and leaves what is above it as it was.

[Mono Bass on the website](/solmara/effects/#mono-bass)

## Controls

Each of the device's controls, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frequency | 20 Hz to 500 Hz | 120 Hz |
| Output | -24 dB to 24 dB | 0 dB |
