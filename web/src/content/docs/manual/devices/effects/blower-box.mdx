**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the one-knob valve character compressors made for thickening drums and bass.

A valve character compressor, for thickening drums, bass, and buses with compression and valve saturation working together: one compress knob that lowers the threshold, steepens the ratio, and brings its own make-up, an attack and a release that scale a detector of two parts, a fast one for hits and a slow one that holds a long loud passage down, and a valve gain stage after it, a triode solved as a circuit, that the saturation knob drives harder without making it louder: second harmonics first, then third as it clips, with its grid drawing current on peaks. A character knob and a voice switch, fat, warm, or bright, for a tone curve after the valve; a mix of what went in for parallel compression, an output, and the automatic make-up on a switch. No one unit: it is the middle of its kind, the one-knob valve compressors and the valve saturation of mixer preamps.
