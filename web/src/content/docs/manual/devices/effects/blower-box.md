A valve character compressor, for thickening drums, bass, and buses with compression and valve saturation working together: one compress knob that lowers the threshold, steepens the ratio, and brings its own make-up, an attack and a release that scale a detector of two parts, a fast one for hits and a slow one that holds a long loud passage down, and a valve gain stage after it, a triode solved as a circuit, that the saturation knob drives harder without making it louder: second harmonics first, then third as it clips, with its grid drawing current on peaks. A character knob and a voice switch, fat, warm, or bright, for a tone curve after the valve; a mix of what went in for parallel compression, an output, and the automatic make-up on a switch. No one unit: it is fitted to the middle of several plugins of the kind, one-knob valve compressors and valve saturators of mixer preamps.

[Blower Box on the website](/solmara/effects/#blower-box)

## Controls

Each of the device's controls, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |

| Release | 0 to 10 | 5 |
| Saturation | 0 to 10 | 3 |
| Character | 0 to 10 | 0 |
| Voice | Fat, Warm, Bright | Fat |
| Mix | 0 linear to 1 linear | 1 linear |

| Auto gain | Off, On | On |
