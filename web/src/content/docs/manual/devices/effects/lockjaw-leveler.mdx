**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1960s American transistor level controller made for podium microphones.

A modelled 1960s podium-microphone level controller, the cheap transistor box on a 9 V battery that locks its output once a voice passes its threshold, made over into a studio processor: crush drives it (a diode shunt turned down by a feedback detector: a fast attack, a release that holds when driven past its range, pumping), distortion drives its output stage into clipping, darkness is a low-pass from 20 kHz to 500 Hz, a slow or fast release, and a mix for parallel crushing.
