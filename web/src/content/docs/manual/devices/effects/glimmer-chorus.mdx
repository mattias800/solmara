**A design of Solmara's own.**

An ensemble chorus: one to four pairs of voices between two delay times, swept by a slow sine and spread across the stereo field, with feedback through a band of the wet sound, from a gentle widening to a ringing, flanging shimmer. Its rate can follow the song's tempo.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Glimmer Chorus. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass Safe**: Width above 250 Hz only: the low end stays where it was, the top spreads.
- **Ringing Ensemble**: Three pairs fed back hard through a band in the middle: the voices ring, near a flanger, for leads that sing.
- **Subtle Doubler**: One pair, a little behind and barely moving: a second take beside the first, for a voice or a guitar.
- **Tempo Swirl**: Two pairs swept once a bar, deep, so the width breathes with the song.
- **Wide Shimmer**: Four pairs of voices between 7 and 16 ms, swept slowly and spread wide: a pad or a pluck made broad and alive.
