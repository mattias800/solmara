**A design of Solmara's own.**

A clipper for the master and for buses, the mastering family's: it drives the audio into a ceiling it never passes, to take the fastest peaks off before a limiter, more cleanly than a limiter can. Its algorithm is how it bends into the ceiling, from hard through soft, sine, a diode pair's and tape's to a valve's lopsided curve, its softness how far under the ceiling it starts, and it runs at once, four, or sixteen times the rate so what it makes does not fold back.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Polarnatt Clipper. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bus

- **Drum Bus Crack**: The tallest drum hits shaved off on a bus, hard and quick, so the bus can come up without its peaks.
- **No Latency**: For recording and playing in: once the rate, so it adds no latency, a soft edge a dB under full scale to catch what would clip.
- **Snare Tame**: A snare's crack rounded by a diode pair's corner: its peak down six dB, its body left.
- **Tape Round**: Tape's long, gentle approach to the ceiling over a broad knee: peaks rounded rather than cut, with a little density, and the output down to match.
- **Valve Warmth**: A valve's lopsided curve, driven, half of it under what came in: even harmonics that thicken a bus or a bass.

### Mastering

- **Loud Master Front**: A modern loud master's trick: a hard edge a dB under full scale, four dB into it, at sixteen times so the edge stays clean; the limiter after it then has little left to do.
- **Peaks Off the Master**: Before Himlavalv on a master: two dB into a soft edge just under full scale, so the sharpest drum peaks are taken off here and the limiter after it works less and pumps less.
- **Transparent Edge**: The gentlest clipping for a master: the sine curve, which comes to the ceiling flat, a broad knee, and sixteen times the rate, so next to nothing folds back.
