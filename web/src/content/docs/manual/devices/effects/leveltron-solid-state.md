**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1960s American solid-state optical leveler that followed the valve one.

A modelled solid-state optical compressor-limiter: a transistor amplifier whose input a photoresistor turns down as a light driven by the audio brightens. Two knobs, how much it turns down and how loud it comes out, a switch from compressing to limiting, and an HF emphasis that has it level the highs more; threshold, ratio, attack, and release come of the light and the cell. Modelled on the transistor successor of the valve leveller, from the late 1960s: the same kind of cell, a faster grip and a brighter, firmer sound, for guitars, drums, bass and vocals.
