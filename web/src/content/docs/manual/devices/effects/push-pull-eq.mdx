**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1950s American passive valve program equaliser that boosts and cuts one band at once.

A modelled passive program equaliser with a valve make-up amplifier: a low shelf that boosts and cuts at once, a peak in the highs with a bandwidth knob, and a high cut, all in one network of pots, capacitors, and a coil, whose sections lean on each other. A switch takes the valves and the transformers out for a clean amplifier at a fraction of the processing. Modelled on the passive valve program equaliser of the 1950s, known for boosting and cutting the lows at once, a lift with a dip above it, for kicks and basses, and for a broad, smooth high boost.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Push-Pull EQ. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Acoustic Guitar, Sparkle**: A peak at 8 kHz for the strings, and less of the body under it.
- **Bass, Round Bottom**: Boost and cut at once at 100 Hz, which fills a bass out and clears the low mids, with the top taken down a little.
- **Dark and Warm**: The high cut from 10 kHz and a little more below: takes the edge off something bright or thin.
- **Kick, Lift and Dip**: The low boost and cut at once at 60 Hz: weight under the kick, a dip above it, and a little click at 4 kHz.
- **Master, Both Ends**: A little more at the very bottom and the very top, and the middle left alone.
- **Mix, Broad Top**: The widest peak at 10 kHz, a couple of dB: a whole mix a little brighter, with no edge to it.
- **Sub Weight**: Boost and cut at once at 30 Hz: the very bottom lifted, and what lies just above it held back.
- **Vocal, Air**: A broad lift at 12 kHz that opens a voice up, with the lows eased off.
