**A design of Solmara's own.**

A stereo imager for the master and for buses, the mastering family's: how wide each of four bands is, from mono through as it came to its side doubled, split where the crossover splits so at every width 1 it changes nothing; and the lows folded to mono below a frequency, as a master's should be.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Trollspegeln Imager. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bus

- **Drum Bus Image**: The kick and the snare kept in the middle, the cymbals and the room as wide as the overheads gave them, a little wider at the top.
- **Narrow Guitars**: A pair of guitars drawn in so they leave room at the edges for the rest, their top left as it was.
- **Stereo Repair**: For a recording whose lows go out of phase between the sides: below 250 Hz made mono, the rest a little narrower.
- **Wide Pads**: Pads and keys spread wide above the low middle, their lows kept narrow so they do not muddy the middle.

### Mastering

- **Focused Middle**: The low middle drawn in toward the middle, where the voice and the snare live, and the top left wide: more focus, the same space.
- **Mono Check**: Everything mono, for listening to how a mix holds up on one speaker; switch it off again before export.
- **Mono Lows**: What every master wants and nothing else: the lows in mono below 120 Hz, so a kick and a bass sit in the middle and a club's system and a vinyl cut take them, everything above as it came.
- **Wider Top**: The top opened a little and the upper middle a touch, the lows mono: a master that breathes wider without its middle going hollow.
