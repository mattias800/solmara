**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1960s American valve optical leveler, the studio's vocal compressor.

A modelled optical levelling amplifier: a valve amplifier whose input a photoresistor turns down as a light driven by the audio brightens. Two knobs, how much it turns down and how loud it comes out, a switch from compressing to limiting, and an emphasis that has it level the highs more; threshold, ratio, attack, and release are not set but come of the light and the cell. Modelled on the two-knob valve leveller of the 1960s that studios put on vocals and bass: slow, smooth, and forgiving. It takes far more processing than the compressor.

## Presets

4 factory presets come with Leveltron Opto. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Drive Only**: No levelling: the amplifier alone, driven a little.
- **Gentle Vocal**: Evens a voice out a few dB, slowly.
- **Hard Limit**: The limit setting, turned well up: loud passages are held down.
- **Steady Bass**: Holds a bass in place; the emphasis keeps its lowest notes from setting it off alone.
