**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1990s American compressor that imitates valves and tape with analogue circuits under digital control.

A modelled character compressor for vocals, drums, bass and rooms: an input knob that drives a fixed threshold and an output after it, attacks from under a tenth of a millisecond to some 40 ms and releases from a twentieth of a second to several, ratios of 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 10 and 20 to 1, each with its own knee, and Nuke, a brickwall; a detector that can hear less of the low end or more of the sibilant band, and a stereo link; a high-pass in the audio; and two harmonic generators, Dist 2, a second harmonic that grows with the reduction, and Dist 3, a clipping stage driven harder for a third. A mix for parallel compression. Modelled on the American digitally controlled analogue compressors of the middle 1990s.

## Presets

6 factory presets come with Duress Compressor. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass, Dist 2**: 4 to 1 with Dist 2: a second harmonic that grows with the reduction, warmth on the loud notes.
- **Drums, Dist 3**: 6 to 1, a quick attack and release, and Dist 3: the clipping stage driven hard, a snare's crack with a third harmonic's grit on it.
- **Opto, Smooth**: 10 to 1, the soft optical kind, a slower attack and release: guitars and keys levelled without the fronts lost.
- **Room, Nuke**: Nuke at the fastest attack, the detector's high-pass in: room microphones flattened so the room swells up between the hits. Blend it in under the kit.
- **Vocal, De-Ess**: 6 to 1 with the band emphasis: the detector hears the esses loudest, so it turns them down most.
- **Vocal, Rock**: 3 to 1 with the detector's high-pass in, a medium attack and release: a lead vocal held level and brought forward. Turn the input up until the meter moves 4 to 8 dB on the loud lines.

| Output | 0 to 10 | 5 |
| Ratio | 1:1, 2:1, 3:1, 4:1, 6:1, 10:1, 20:1, Nuke | 4:1 |
| Detector | Normal, HP, Band emphasis, HP and emphasis | Normal |
| Stereo link | Off, On | Off |
| Audio HP | Off, On | Off |

| Mix | 0% to 100% | 100% |
