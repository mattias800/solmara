**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the bus compressor of the 1980s British large-format consoles.

A modelled bus compressor built on a voltage-controlled amplifier, for gluing a mix bus or a drum bus: a threshold, ratios of 2, 4, and 10 to 1, an attack of 0.1 to 30 ms and a release of 0.1 to 1.2 s on switches, with an automatic release that lets a short peak go quickly and a long passage slowly, and a make-up gain. One peak detector serves both channels, so the stereo image holds still. Its timing is the circuit's network of a diode, resistances, and capacitors, solved exactly. With a high-pass in the sidechain, a mix of the compressed with what went in, a switch for where the detector listens, ahead of the gain element or behind it, and a sidechain from another track. Modelled on the stereo compressor in the centre section of a large British mixing console of the 1980s.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Desk Glue Bus Compressor. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Drum Bus, Punch**: 4 to 1 with the slowest attack and the fastest release: each hit's front passes before it takes hold, and it lets go before the next, so the drums come out harder.
- **Drum Bus, Tight**: 4 to 1 at 3 ms and 0.3 s, with the lows out of the sidechain: the kit is held in one place, the room is brought up a little, and the kick does not pump the cymbals.
- **Drum Room, Breathing**: 10 to 1 with the detector behind the gain element, as the console's own is, which takes hold sooner the higher the ratio, at 1 ms and the fastest release: a room microphone pumps and breathes between the hits.
- **Ducked by the Kick**: 4 to 1, the fastest attack, and 0.3 s of release, for a sidechain: set it to the kick's track, and a bass or a pad is pushed down each time the kick hits and swells back between.
- **Master, Touch**: 2 to 1, the slowest attack, the automatic release, and a high threshold: it works only on the loudest moments, a dB at the most. The last device on the master.
- **Mix, Glue**: 2 to 1 with the slowest attack and the automatic release: the whole mix is held together by a dB or two, the fronts of the hits pass, and a loud passage is let go slowly. Lower the threshold until the meter moves 1 to 3 dB.
- **Mix, Low End Free**: The glue with a high-pass of 120 Hz in the sidechain, so the kick and the bass do not pull the rest of the mix down with them, and a faster attack of 10 ms.
- **Parallel Smash**: 10 to 1 with both switches at their fastest and the threshold low, everything flattened, and the mix at 40 %: the crushed drums under the drums as they were, in one device.
