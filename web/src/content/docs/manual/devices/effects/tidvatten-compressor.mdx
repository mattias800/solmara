**A design of Solmara's own.**

A three-band upward and downward compressor, for sound design and for loud, dense sounds: each band is pushed down above its push line and lifted below its lift line at once, so what is loud comes down and what is quiet comes up, with Depth for how much of it is heard and Time for how fast it moves. Vital's multiband compressor imports as it.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Tidvatten Compressor. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bus

- **Drum Bus Smash**: Drums squashed fast, the hits pushed down hard and the room between them lifted, at a little over half its depth.
- **Vocal Up Front**: A voice's body held even and its quiet words lifted, its top pushed only gently: closer, steadier, never harsh.

### Sound design

- **Air and Detail**: Mostly lifting, and mostly in the top: breaths, noise and the tails of notes brought up, the hits hardly touched.
- **All the Way**: Every band pushed down and lifted at full depth: the loud and the quiet brought to one level in each band, the dense, bright, in-your-face sound of a synth or a drop run through it.
- **Bass Thickener**: A bass held to one level from its lows to its growl, the top left alone: every note as loud as the next, its harmonics brought forward.
- **Half Tide**: The same at half its depth, under what came in: more detail and sheen without losing the hits' shape.
- **Lift Only**: Nothing pushed down, only the quiet lifted, at half its depth: tails, reverb and sustain brought up under the notes, the attacks as they were.
- **Pad Glow**: Slow and soft: a pad's movement evened out over a long time, its quiet parts glowing up between the chords.
