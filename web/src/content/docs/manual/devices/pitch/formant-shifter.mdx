**An emulation of a digital original,** modelled on the vocal transformers of 2010s pop and hip-hop.

A modelled vocal pitch and formant shifter in the style of the vocal transformers of 2010s pop and hip-hop, for vocals, raps and spoken lines: the pitch moved up to an octave either way and the formants, the voice's size, moved apart from it, a period at a time (TD-PSOLA), so a voice keeps its tone. Transpose moves it, Quantize first draws it to the nearest semitone, Robot holds it on one note; Link moves the formants with the pitch; a valve-like Drive and a Mix. For one voice at a time. It plays 50 ms late, which the mix makes up for.
