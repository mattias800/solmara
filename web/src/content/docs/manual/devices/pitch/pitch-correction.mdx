**A design of Solmara's own.**

Corrects a voice's pitch as it plays: the pitch is heard every 5 ms and moved to the nearest note of the project's scale guide, or of a scale chosen here. The retune speed is how fast it gets there: at 0 the voice jumps from note to note and its vibrato is gone, the effect known from records; at 50 to 100 ms it sounds sung in tune, its scoops and vibrato kept. Humanize retunes a held note more slowly, amount corrects part of the way, transpose and formant move the voice and its vowels' color, and detune aims at another tuning than A 440. The voice is made again a period at a time (TD-PSOLA), which keeps its tone; where no pitch is heard it passes as it came. For one voice at a time: a vocal, a lead, a bass line. It plays 30 to 60 ms late, by its range, which the mix makes up for.

## Appendix: every control

Each of the device's controls by the name automation and the inspector give it, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scale | 15 choices: Scale guide, Major, Minor, Dorian, … | Scale guide |
| Key | 12 choices: C, C#, D, D#, … | C |
| Retune speed | 0 ms to 400 ms | 20 ms |
| Humanize | 0% to 100% | 0% |
| Amount | 0% to 100% | 100% |
| Transpose | -12 semitones to 12 semitones | 0 semitones |
| Formant | -6 semitones to 6 semitones | 0 semitones |
| Detune | -50 cents to 50 cents | 0 cents |
| Mix | 0% to 100% | 100% |
| Range | High voice, Voice, Low voice | Voice |
