**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the underground echo chambers of a 1950s Hollywood studio.

A studio echo chamber modeled as a room: a bare room of shellacked concrete under the studio, a loudspeaker at one end fed from the console's echo send, and two microphones whose signals come back as the return. Each of its four rooms has its size, its walls and its speaker; the time each band rings comes from them and from the air, which takes the highs on every meter, and the first reflections are the speaker's images in the walls, heard where the microphones stand. Four pairs of microphones (valve omnis, ribbons, condenser omnis, valve cardioids), moved from the speaker to the far end; decay hangs absorbent in the room, down to about a second; pre-delay and a low cut on the send, three bands and width on the return, mix and output. Modeled on the underground chambers of a 1950s Hollywood studio.
