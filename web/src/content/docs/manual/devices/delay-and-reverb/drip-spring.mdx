**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the early-1960s American outboard spring reverb tank of surf guitar.

A spring reverb tank of the kind built into guitar amplifiers and sold as studio units, modelled as its springs: two or three long coil springs, each driven at one end and heard at the other, each a bank of modes of one kind of wave whose speed falls as its pitch rises, so a hit comes out as a chirp, lows first, and comes back as a longer one each time it has been to the far end and back: the drip and the boing of surf guitar. Dwell drives the valve amplifier that drives the tank, and a loud transient overdrives it into a crash; tone is a shelf on what comes back. Decay, a short or a long tank, two or three springs panned across the sides, pre-delay, width, mix, and output. No latency.
