**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the mid-century rotating-speaker organ cabinet, its horn and its drum.

A modeled rotating speaker cabinet, built from its mechanism: a valve amplifier that overdrives into a crossover near 800 Hz, a horn turning above it and a drum below, slow, fast or stopped, each reaching its speed in its own time; the pitch moved by each mouth's Doppler shift, the level by where it points, and what it sends back off the cabinet's walls; two microphones, their spread and distance. For organs, guitars, electric pianos and synths. Switching speed while it plays sweeps between them.
