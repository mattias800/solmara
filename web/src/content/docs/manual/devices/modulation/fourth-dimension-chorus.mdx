**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the late-1970s Japanese studio rack chorus with four push-buttons.

A modeled studio dimension chorus, for pads, keys, clean guitars, vocals, and whole mixes: two bucket-brigade lines that one slow triangle sweeps in opposite directions, each mixed mostly into the other side and a little, turned over, into its own, beside the dry signal, so a sound widens and thickens without the wobble of a chorus. Four buttons, pressed alone or together as the unit allows: 1 and 2 sweep slowly, 2 the deeper, 3 and 4 faster and shallower, and 4 also mixes each line into its own side nearly as much as into the other, for the widest image; summed to mono, the lines then cancel. Modeled on the late-1970s Japanese studio rack unit.
