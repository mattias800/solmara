**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the chorus of the early-1980s Japanese six-voice polysynths.

A modeled bucket-brigade chorus, for synths, pads, and clean guitars: two delay lines of 256 stages, each a chain of capacitors that a clock passes the signal along, so that the delay is the clock's rate. One triangle sweeps the two clocks in opposite directions, one line's delay growing as the other's shrinks, and the dry signal is mixed beside them, which spreads a mono sound wide. Two buttons, as the unit has: I sweeps slowly, II faster, and I and II together is a quick, shallow vibrato in both lines at once. The lines sample what comes in at their clock, so their images are heard on high notes, and their transfer adds a second harmonic and a little third. Rate and depth against the mode's own, a mix, a width, a stereo or summed input, drive, the lines' noise, and an output. Modeled on the chorus built into the Japanese six-voice polysynths of the early 1980s.
