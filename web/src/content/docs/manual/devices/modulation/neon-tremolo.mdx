**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the optical tremolo of the 1960s American guitar amplifiers.

A modeled optical tremolo of the American amplifier kind, for guitars, electric pianos, and organs: a valve oscillator drives a neon lamp sealed with a photocell, and the cell turns the signal down as the lamp lights it. The lamp flashes once a swing, the intensity setting how brightly, and the cell darkens fast and lets go slowly, so the throb is not a sine: it falls in a few milliseconds and climbs back over tens of them. Speed and intensity, as the amplifier has, and an output. Both sides through one cell. Modeled on the tremolo of the black-panel American combos of the mid-1960s.
