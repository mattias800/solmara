**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on one voice of the early-1980s Japanese small analogue drum machine.

One channel of the PH-6 alone, for a pad of a drum rack: the tabs on its face choose which, the bass drum, snare, low and high toms, cymbal and hats, each keeping its own knobs; any key strikes it.

## Presets

32 factory presets come with PH-6 Part. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bass drum

- **Click Kick**: The kick at its shortest and highest, mostly click.
- **High Kick**: The kick tuned up, nearer a tom.
- **Low Kick**: The kick tuned down.
- **Machine Kick**: The smaller machine's kick as it has it: a short, round thump with a click on top.
- **Pillow Kick**: The kick soft and long, no click.
- **Ringing Thump**: The kick left to ring, deeper and longer.
- **Short Thump**: The kick cut short: a dry thump for busy patterns.
- **Soft Kick**: The kick with its click rounded off.

### Closed hat

- **Loose Hat**: The closed hat let ring.
- **Machine Closed Hat**: The smaller machine's closed hat as it has it: a quick, bright tick.
- **Tick Hat**: The closed hat at its shortest.

### Cymbal

- **Dark Ride**: The cymbal's slow band alone, long, nearer a ride's swell.
- **Machine Cymbal**: The smaller machine's cymbal as it has it: a bright splash over a slower ring.

### High tom

- **Low Bongo**: The high tom tuned up and cut short, nearer a bongo.
- **Machine High Tom**: The smaller machine's high tom as it has it.
- **Rack Tom**: The high tom tuned up and left to ring.
- **Short High Tom**: The high tom cut short.

### Low tom

- **Deep Tom**: The low tom at the bottom of its tune.
- **Floor Tom**: The low tom tuned down and left to ring.
- **Machine Low Tom**: The smaller machine's low tom as it has it: it starts high and falls to its pitch.
- **Short Low Tom**: The low tom cut short.

### Open hat

- **Long Open Hat**: The open hat at its longest.
- **Machine Open Hat**: The smaller machine's open hat as it has it: a hiss that holds and stops.
- **Short Open Hat**: The open hat cut short.

### Snare drum

- **Body Snare**: The snare mostly shell, its noise turned down.
- **High Snare**: The snare tuned up, nearer a piccolo.
- **Low Snare**: The snare tuned down.
- **Machine Snare**: The smaller machine's snare as it has it: a light shell and a short burst of noise.
- **Noise Burst**: The snare almost all noise, ringing long.
- **Snappy Snare**: The snare with its noise brought up.
- **Tight Snare**: The snare's noise cut short.
- **Washy Snare**: The snare's noise left to hiss on.
