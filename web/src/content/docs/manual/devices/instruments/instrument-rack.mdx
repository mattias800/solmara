**A design of Solmara's own.**

Several instruments played as one. Each chain holds an instrument, with MIDI devices before it and audio effects after it, and has a level, a pan, a mute, and a solo. Every chain plays every note, for layers, unless it is given a range of keys or of velocities, for a split keyboard or a sound that changes with how hard a key is struck.

## Presets

20 factory presets come with Instrument Rack. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Keys

- **Ballad Organ Rotary**: The organ's Ballad Jazz, 88 8000 000 with soft, slow percussion, the scanner off and the rotary turning slowly: late-night chords that breathe.
- **Bell Tines**: The tine piano's Seventies Stage with the FM synth's Glass Bell 10 dB under it: the bright, glassy attack of the layered electric pianos of the 1980s ballad, the tines' warmth under it.
- **Felt Piano and Pad**: The reed piano's Felt Soft over the five-voice synth's Warm Pad 8 dB down: a mellow, close piano with a slow analogue pad blooming behind each chord.
- **Gospel Organ and Piano**: The organ's Soul Comping, 68 8600 000 with the third's percussion, through the rotary turning slowly, and the tine piano's Gospel Bark 5 dB under it: the church band's organ and piano on one keyboard.
- **Gospel Organ Rotary**: Every drawbar out, as the organ's Full Gospel, the scanner off and the rotary at speed: the choir's organ in full cry.
- **Jazz Organ Rotary**: The organ's Jazz Trio registration, 83 8000 000 with the third's percussion, into the rotary cabinet turning slowly.
- **Rock Organ Rotary**: The organ's Rock Drive, 88 8800 000 with the preamp pushed, into the rotary's amplifier driven hard and the rotors fast: the growl of a rock band's organ.
- **Tines and Strings**: The tine piano's Soft Ballad with the string machine's Classic Strings swelling in some 6 dB under it: the piano speaks first and the strings hold the chord after it.

### Lead

- **Octave Lead Stack**: The three-oscillator monosynth's Fat Lead with the semi-modular's Sync Lead an octave over it, 4 dB under: a fat lead with a sync edge riding on top.
- **Trance Lead Stack**: The flagship synth's Anthem Supersaw Lead and the two-part synth's Anthem Superstring just under it, panned a little apart: two big saw stacks as one wide trance lead.

### Orchestral

- **Keyboard Orchestra**: Three zones: the string machine's Contrabass Line under C3, the dual-channel synth's Bowed Strings from C3 to B4, and the FM synth's Flute Lead from C5 up: bass, harmony and melody of a small orchestra under two hands.
- **Strings and Horns**: The string machine's Full Orchestra with the eight-voice synth's Soft Brass 6 dB under it: horns that swell inside a whole string section.
- **Strings into Brass**: The string machine's Classic Strings on every key, and the dual-channel synth's Cosmos Brass joining only as a key is struck hard, faded in over 30 steps of velocity: play softly for strings, dig in for horns.

### Pad

- **Pluck over Pad**: The eight-voice synth's Synth Pluck on top of the Warm Analog Pad 4 dB under it: each chord picked bright and dying away while the pad swells up and holds it.
- **Vibes over Velvet**: The FM synth's Vibraphone struck over the Nordic synth's Sammet, a velvet pad: a mallet's ring on top of a dark, soft bed.

### Split

- **Acid Bass and Lead**: Split at C3: the acid bass synth's Classic Squelch in the left hand, the two-part synth's Euro Octave Lead in the right: a squelching line under a rave lead.
- **Funk Bass and Clav**: Split at C3: the three-oscillator monosynth's Funk Bass in the left hand, the electric clavichord's Funk Both Pickups in the right: bass and rhythm keys of a funk band.
- **Organ with Left-Hand Bass**: Split at C3: the three-oscillator monosynth's Round Bass in the left hand, the tonewheel organ's Blues Shuffle, 88 8000 008 with the drive, through the rotary turning slowly in the right: the organist's own bass line under a bluesy right hand.
- **Synthwave Split**: Split at C3: the two-part synth's Synthwave Bass in the left hand, the flagship synth's Dreamy Eighties Pad in the right: a driving eighth-note bass under a wide 1980s pad.
- **Upright and Tines**: Split at C3: the FM synth's Upright Bass in the left hand, the tine piano's Jazz Mellow in the right with its bass boost taken back from 7 to 3, so the piano leaves the low end to the bass: a jazz trio's bass and piano under two hands.
