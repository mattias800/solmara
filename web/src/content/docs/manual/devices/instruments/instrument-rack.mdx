**A design of Solmara's own.**

Several instruments played as one. Each chain holds an instrument, with MIDI devices before it and audio effects after it, and has a level, a pan, a mute, and a solo. Every chain plays every note, for layers, unless it is given a range of keys or of velocities, for a split keyboard or a sound that changes with how hard a key is struck.

## Appendix: the factory presets

Instrument Rack comes with 20 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Keys:** Ballad Organ Rotary, Bell Tines, Felt Piano and Pad, Gospel Organ and Piano, Gospel Organ Rotary, Jazz Organ Rotary, Rock Organ Rotary, Tines and Strings.
- **Lead:** Octave Lead Stack, Trance Lead Stack.
- **Orchestral:** Keyboard Orchestra, Strings and Horns, Strings into Brass.
- **Pad:** Pluck over Pad, Vibes over Velvet.
- **Split:** Acid Bass and Lead, Funk Bass and Clav, Organ with Left-Hand Bass, Synthwave Split, Upright and Tines.
