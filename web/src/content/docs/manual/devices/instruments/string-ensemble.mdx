**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1970s string machines, their bass and upper sections through a rich ensemble.

A modelled string ensemble keyboard of the 1970s, fitted to a reference by measurement: a divide-down organ, every key always running from one master clock, keyed through its own crescendo and sustain; violin, viola, trumpet and horn on every key and contrabass and cello on the lower keys, each register a fixed filter; and the ensemble, three bucket-brigade lines swept by a slow and a fast oscillator, in mono or stereo. A choir register and an LFO for vibrato and tremolo, which its reference adds.

## Presets

17 factory presets come with String Ensemble. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bass

- **Contrabass Line**: The bass section alone, contrabass and cello, short and dry for a line.

### Brass

- **Brass Ensemble**: Trumpet and horn through the ensemble, a quick swell.
- **Horns and Strings**: The horn tab over the viola and the cello, a medium swell: a soft horn section doubled by strings, for held chords.
- **Trumpet Line**: The trumpet tab alone with a quick swell and a little delayed vibrato: a bright, buzzing line for a melody.

### Choir

- **Choir and Strings**: The choir over violin and viola and the cello below, a slow swell and a long release: a lush pad for a ballad's chorus.
- **Slow Choir**: The choir over the viola, swelling slowly, a touch of vibrato.

### Pads

- **Classic Strings**: The string machine pad: violin and viola, a long swell and release, the ensemble wide.
- **Dry Divider**: The ensemble off: the machine's raw divider saws, bright and still, nearer an organ than strings.
- **Full Orchestra**: Every string at once, contrabass and cello under violin and viola, swelling in.
- **Init**: Violin and viola over the cello, a slow swell, the ensemble on in stereo.
- **Mono Ensemble**: The ensemble's mono output: the chorus's movement without its width, as the early machines gave it.
- **Space Wash**: The longest swell and release with the whole section: a slow wash of strings for space rock and ambient.
- **Tremolo Pad**: Violin and viola through a square-wave tremolo at a medium speed: a pad that pulses in time.

### Strings

- **Disco Strings**: Violin and viola with almost no swell and a short release, the ensemble wide: the quick, bright string stabs and runs of a disco record.
- **Vibrato Strings**: Violin and viola with a vibrato that waits a moment after the first key and fades in: held chords that come alive.
- **Viola Section**: The viola alone through the ensemble: darker and warmer than the violins, for chords in the middle.
- **Violins High**: The violin alone, an octave over the viola, with a medium swell: a thin, high line over a band.
