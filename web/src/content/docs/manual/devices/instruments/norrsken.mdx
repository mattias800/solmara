**An emulation of a digital original,** modelled on the late-1990s Scandinavian virtual-analogue keyboard in its red case, with morphs on every knob.

A modelled twenty-voice virtual-analogue synthesizer from Stockholm, of the turn of the century: its digital engine held to a reference by measurement and blind listening: two oscillators with sync, linear FM and ring modulation, a filter of five types with distortion, three envelopes, two LFOs (the second an arpeggiator or an echo), velocity and wheel morphs on every knob, unison, and the wheel, the stick, aftertouch and a pedal as the hardware takes them. Imports the hardware's documented SysEx programs.

### Bass

- **Djuphav**: The deep sea: a sine with a quiet triangle an octave above it, the octave shift taking it down one more. Play a slow line under everything. Push the wheel to morph the triangle louder for a little more edge on small speakers.
- **Draug**: A restless dead: two saws a little out of tune, beating slowly, through the distortion and a 24 dB low-pass that LFO 2 moves. Hold long notes at the bottom. Push the wheel to morph the beating faster and the filter open.
- **Fenris**: The bound wolf: at rest a dark sub. Push the wheel to morph it loose: the cutoff and resonance rise, the pulse below growls into the saw by FM, and LFO 1 starts to wobble the filter in eighth notes, in time with the song. Play long notes at the bottom and ride the wheel.
- **Granit**: Granite: four saws in unison on every key, an octave apart, spread wide through a 24 dB low-pass with a firm envelope. Play a line at the bottom. Strike harder to morph the envelope deeper; the wheel opens the filter.
- **Gruva**: A mine: two pulses whose widths LFO 1 sweeps, through the notch and low-pass, which carves a hollow out of them. Play a line at the bottom. Push the wheel to morph the sweep quicker and the filter open.
- **Hammare**: A hammer: a pulse and a saw an octave down. Soft notes are round and dark, hard ones bright and biting, the morph opening the cutoff, the envelope and the resonance with the strike. Play a line at the bottom and dig in; the wheel opens the filter.
- **Irrbloss**: A will-o'-the-wisp: one saw through a 24 dB low-pass at the edge of whistling, its envelope following the strike, the distortion on, gliding only where notes overlap. Play a line at the bottom, slurring some notes. Strike harder to morph the filter brighter and the glide longer; the wheel opens the filter.
- **Kolsvart**: Pitch black: oscillator 2 synced and swept down by the mod envelope at each note, through a 24 dB low-pass. Play a line at the bottom. Strike harder to morph the sweep wider; the wheel raises the synced oscillator for a growl.
- **Malm**: Ore: a sine and its ring modulation with a narrow pulse an octave up, through a low-pass that snaps shut. Play a line at the bottom. Strike harder to morph the pulse narrower and the filter brighter; the wheel moves the pulse's pitch for clangorous bends.
- **Skridsko**: A skate: a pulse with a saw an octave up, gliding wherever notes overlap, through a resonant low-pass. Play a line at the bottom, its notes touching. Strike harder to morph the glide longer; the wheel opens the filter.
- **Smide**: Forged iron: a sine frequency-modulated by a triangle at its own pitch, the mod envelope striking a burst of FM at each note that the hammer leaves ringing. Play a line at the bottom. Strike harder to morph the burst brighter; the wheel adds FM.
- **Torv**: Peat: a saw and a square an octave down, plucked through a 12 dB low-pass that closes fast, its envelope following the strike. Play a line at the bottom. Strike harder to morph it ringing longer; the wheel opens the filter.
