**An emulation of a digital original,** modelled on the sound chip of the best-selling home computer of the early 1980s: three voices and a filter.

A three-voice chip synth with clocked oscillators and ADSR, pulse width, sync, ring modulation, and a shared filter. Poly or unison; two chip models and PAL/NTSC clocks. An approximate SID sound engine.

[SID on the website](/solmara/instruments/sid/)

## Appendix: the factory presets

SID comes with 32 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Arpeggio:** Arp Pluck, Octave Arp.
- **Bass:** Filter Bass, Growl Bass, Pluck Bass, Pulse Bass, Sync Bass, Triangle Sub Bass.
- **Bell:** Chip Xylophone, Ring Chime, Struck Ring Bell.
- **Chord:** Major Chord, Minor Chord.
- **FX:** Low Blast, Machine Drone.
- **Keys:** Calliope, Chip Harpsichord, Chip Piano, Square Start.
- **Lead:** Breathy Triangle Lead, Combined Wave Lead, Narrow Lead, Ring Lead, Square Lead, Sync Lead.
- **Pad:** Beating Pulse Pad, Dark Saw Pad, Pulse Pad.
- **Percussion:** Noise Snare, Noise Tick, Open Hat, Thump Kick.
