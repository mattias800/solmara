**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on one voice of the mid-1980s Japanese analogue and sampled drum machine.

One channel of the PH-9 alone, for a pad of a drum rack: the tabs on its face choose which, the bass drum, snare, three toms, rim shot, clap, hats, crash and ride, each keeping its own knobs; any key strikes it.

## Appendix: the factory presets

PH-9 Part comes with 48 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass drum:** Big Sweep, Deep Kick, High Kick, Long Kick, Machine Kick, Punch Kick, Short Kick, Soft Kick.
- **Closed hat:** Machine Closed Hat, Tight Hat.
- **Crash:** Dark Crash, Machine Crash.
- **Hand clap:** Big Clap, Bright Clap, Dark Clap, Dry Clap, Machine Clap, Room Clap, Snap, Thin Clap.
- **High tom:** Machine High Tom, Short High Tom, Zap Tom.
- **Low tom:** Long Low Tom, Machine Low Tom.
- **Mid tom:** Flat Tom, Machine Mid Tom, Noisy Tom.
- **Open hat:** Long Open Hat, Machine Open Hat.
- **Ride:** Bright Ride, Machine Ride.
- **Rim shot:** Block, High Rim, Low Rim, Machine Rim Shot, Ringing Rim, Short Rim, Tick Rim, Wood Rim.
- **Snare drum:** Body Snare, High Snare, Long Snare, Low Snare, Machine Snare, Noise Snare, Snappy Snare, Tight Snare.
