**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on one voice of the mid-1980s Japanese analogue and sampled drum machine.

One channel of the PH-9 alone, for a pad of a drum rack: the tabs on its face choose which, the bass drum, snare, three toms, rim shot, clap, hats, crash and ride, each keeping its own knobs; any key strikes it.

## Presets

48 factory presets come with PH-9 Part. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bass drum

- **Big Sweep**: A long, deep fall of pitch from high above.
- **Deep Kick**: Lower and longer.
- **High Kick**: Its pitch settled higher.
- **Long Kick**: The kick let ring.
- **Machine Kick**: The later machine's kick as it has it: a click, a fast fall of pitch, and a long round boom.
- **Punch Kick**: More click and a shorter boom.
- **Short Kick**: The kick cut short.
- **Soft Kick**: The kick with its attack at nothing.

### Closed hat

- **Machine Closed Hat**: The later machine's closed hat as it has it: a bright, quick tick.
- **Tight Hat**: The closed hat at its shortest.

### Crash

- **Dark Crash**: The crash played slower: lower and longer.
- **Machine Crash**: The later machine's crash as it has it.

### Hand clap

- **Big Clap**: A little lower, with a long room.
- **Bright Clap**: Its noise's band moved up.
- **Dark Clap**: Its noise's band moved down.
- **Dry Clap**: The clap with the least of its room.
- **Machine Clap**: The later machine's clap as it has it: three quick claps and a short room.
- **Room Clap**: The clap with the most of its room.
- **Snap**: A dry, bright snap.
- **Thin Clap**: High and dry.

### High tom

- **Machine High Tom**: The later machine's high tom as it has it.
- **Short High Tom**: The high tom cut short.
- **Zap Tom**: The high tom falling far and fast.

### Low tom

- **Long Low Tom**: The low tom let ring.
- **Machine Low Tom**: The later machine's low tom as it has it.

### Mid tom

- **Flat Tom**: The mid tom with no fall of pitch.
- **Machine Mid Tom**: The later machine's mid tom as it has it.
- **Noisy Tom**: The mid tom with more noise in its start.

### Open hat

- **Long Open Hat**: The open hat let ring.
- **Machine Open Hat**: The later machine's open hat as it has it: a wash that falls away.

### Ride

- **Bright Ride**: The ride played faster: higher and shorter.
- **Machine Ride**: The later machine's ride as it has it.

### Rim shot

- **Block**: Lower and longer, nearer a wood block.
- **High Rim**: Tuned up.
- **Low Rim**: Tuned down.
- **Machine Rim Shot**: The later machine's rim shot as it has it: a short, hard knock.
- **Ringing Rim**: Its resonators let ring.
- **Short Rim**: Shorter still.
- **Tick Rim**: As high and short as it goes.
- **Wood Rim**: Low and ringing.

### Snare drum

- **Body Snare**: Mostly shell, rung longer.
- **High Snare**: The shells tuned up.
- **Long Snare**: The noise let ring.
- **Low Snare**: The shells tuned down.
- **Machine Snare**: The later machine's snare as it has it: two shells and a bright noise.
- **Noise Snare**: All noise, the shell barely there.
- **Snappy Snare**: More of the bright noise.
- **Tight Snare**: Short noise and a short shell.
