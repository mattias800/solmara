**A design of Solmara's own.**

Pads, each for one key, each with an instrument and effects of its own: a kit built a sound at a time. A pad gives its instrument middle C, so a sample sounds as it was recorded on any pad, and pads of one choke group cut each other off, as a closed hi-hat cuts an open one: the pad's whole output fades down in 5 ms, including its effects. A mouse strike is softer at the top and harder at the bottom.

[Drum Rack on the website](/solmara/instruments/drum-rack/)
