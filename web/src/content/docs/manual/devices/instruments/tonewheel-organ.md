**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the mid-century American tonewheel console organ, with drawbars, percussion and chorus-vibrato.

A modelled tonewheel organ of the late 1950s, built from its mechanism: 91 tonewheels on one motor through twelve gear ratios, so its tuning is near equal temperament and not on it; nine drawbars on each of two manuals and two on the pedals, with the foldback at the ends; contacts that close apart and bounce, which is the key click; the wiring's leakage; the percussion of the second or third harmonic, soft or normal, fast or slow, struck only when no key is held; the scanner vibrato and chorus V1 to C3; the preamp's drive and the expression pedal. Every key sounds at once.
