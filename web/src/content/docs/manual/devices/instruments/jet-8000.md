A ten-voice, two-part digital synthesizer: Super Saw and six other oscillator modes, sync, cross and ring modulation, a 12/24 dB multimode filter, three envelopes, two LFOs, Unison, velocity destinations, multi-effects and delay, the ribbon and the arpeggiator. Imports original patches and performances. Another track's audio is its external input, which OSC2 External and the Voice Modulator play.

[Jet-8000 on the website](/solmara/instruments/jet-8000/)
