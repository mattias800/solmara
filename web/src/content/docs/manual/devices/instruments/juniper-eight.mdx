**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the early-1980s Japanese eight-voice flagship polysynth, two oscillators a voice, split or layered.

A modelled eight-voice polysynth of the early 1980s, built from its circuit and held to a reference by measurement: each voice two oscillators, the first with its triangle, saw, pulse and square and cross-modulation from the second, the second with its sine, saw, pulse and noise, sync either way, a source mix into a high-pass and a four-pole low-pass switchable to two poles that sings at full resonance, and an amplifier, on two envelopes that follow the keys if asked. An LFO with its delay to the oscillators, the pulse width, the filter and the amplifier. An upper and a lower patch, played whole, dual or split; solo, unison and two poly modes; hold, portamento and an arpeggiator.

- **Synth-Pop Arpeggio**: The arpeggiator up two octaves in sixteenths with the song, each step a pulse plucked through a resonant filter: the chiming arpeggio of early 1980s synth-pop.
- **Synthwave Arpeggio**: After MusicRadar's walkthrough of an arpeggio in the original's style on an emulation of the instrument: the arpeggiator up and down, VCO-1's pulse a little narrow, VCO-2's saw an octave up and quieter, the filter near 300 Hz with a little resonance, ENV-1 short with a little sustain, a hair of slow LFO on VCO-2. From the article: the mode, the waves, the width, the octave, the mix, the cutoff, the envelope's shape and the LFO; estimated: the range, the rate in time with the song and their conversion to the panel.
- **Triplet Arpeggio**: The arpeggiator up and down two octaves in eighth-note triplets, each step a square plucked bright.

### Split

- **Arpeggio and Strings**: Split at G under middle C: the arpeggiator runs the bass notes up two octaves in sixteenths as plucks, the string ensemble held over them.
- **Bass and Brass**: One of the factory setups listed on the maker's 1981 patch sheets for the original: the upright bass under the split and the synth brass over it, each read off its sheet (the panel's settings were measured off the sheet's drawing, to about half a step; estimated: the split point, at G under middle C).
- **Bass and Pad**: Split at G under middle C: the warm pad over it and a round square bass under it.
- **Bass and Strings**: Split at G under middle C: the strings over it and a plucked bass under it, each patch on four voices.
- **Brass over Strings**: Dual: both patches on every key, the synth brass's swell over the strings' slow bed.
- **Lead over Bass**: Split at G under middle C: a bright sync lead over it and a plucked bass under it, for playing a line and its bass at once.
- **Octave Brass Layer**: After Nick Magnus's analogue brass in Sound On Sound, where a big-band section is the brass with a second layer an octave up: dual, the synth brass on the upper patch and the lower an octave over it, a little quicker and quieter. The layering is the article's; the values are estimated.
- **Organ and Bass**: Split at G under middle C: a square-and-octave organ over it, gated, and a round bass under it.
- **Pluck and Pad**: Dual: a bright pluck strikes each chord and dies away while a slow pad swells in under it.
- **Strings and Bell**: Dual: the string ensemble with a gently cross-modulated chime struck on each key, ringing over the bow. Deeper, the cross-modulation would raise the chime a semitone over the strings.
- **Strings and Tines**: A dual pairing suggested on the maker's 1981 factory patch sheet for the low strings: the low strings over the mellow electric piano on every key, each read off its sheet (the settings were measured off the drawings, to about half a step; estimated: the balance).

### Strings

- **Bright Strings**: After Francesco Preve's sound-design article for the maker's software model of the original, on the maker's site: VCO-1 at 4' and VCO-2's pulse an octave over the keys, six cents sharp, the width set a little by hand, the high-pass near 3 and the low-pass open near 8.4, ENV-2 bowing in slowly and letting go slowly. From the article: the feet, the waves, the detune, the width, both filters and ENV-2's times; estimated: their conversion to the panel. Its chorus is left out: the instrument has none.
- **Chamber Strings**: After Nick Magnus's string patches in Sound On Sound, where the eight-voice polysynth's strings are two saws with a fast vibrato of some 8 Hz on one: VCO-2 shaken by the LFO through the two-pole with a touch of resonance and a gentle bow. From the article: the two saws and the vibrato's rate and target; estimated: the rest.
- **High Strings**: Read off the maker's 1981 factory patch sheet for the original's high strings, every setting measured off the sheet's drawing to about half a step. A saw and a pulse at 4', VCO-2 a little sharp, the LFO swinging the width and VCO-1's pitch, through the two-pole well open with some of ENV-2: the violins over the low strings.
- **Low Strings**: Read off the maker's 1981 factory patch sheet for the original's low strings, its best-known string sound, every setting measured off the sheet's drawing to about half a step; estimated: VCO-1's vibrato, drawn near 1.5 and set at 1 here, where the model's depth law is steeper at its foot. A saw and a pulse at 16', VCO-2 a little sharp, the LFO swinging the width and VCO-1's pitch, through the two-pole open with ENV-1 on it, both envelopes slow and shorter higher up.
- **Mellow Strings**: Read off the maker's 1981 factory patch sheet for the original's mellow strings, every setting measured off the sheet's drawing to about half a step; estimated: VCO-1's vibrato, drawn near 2 and set at 1.2 here, where the model's depth law is steeper at its foot. A saw and a pulse at 8', the LFO swinging the width and VCO-1's pitch, through the two-pole open and still: a soft section.
- **PWM Strings**: After Gordon Reid's string-machine patch on a Japanese polysynth in Sound On Sound's Synth Secrets: both oscillators' pulses swept from square to thin by the LFO, close in pitch, VCO-2 an octave down, the filter half shut with no envelope and some key follow, the level a slow trapezoid. From the article: the pulses and their sweep, the octave, the filter and the trapezoid; estimated: the values. Its random LFO on VCO-1 is left out: the instrument has one LFO, here on the width.
- **Solo Violin**: After Gordon Reid's bowed-string patch in Sound On Sound's Synth Secrets: a saw at 4' with the low-pass lowered and the high-pass raised, a slow bow, a vibrato of some 6 Hz that waits before it comes in, and a glide just off its foot, played solo. From the article: the saw at 4', the two filters' directions, the attack, the delayed vibrato and the glide; estimated: the values. Its formants are left out: the instrument has no band filters.
- **String Ensemble**: After Gordon Reid's string patch on a later eight-voice polysynth in Sound On Sound's Synth Secrets: two saws at 8', VCO-2 four cents flat, a light LFO on VCO-1, the low-pass near 4.7 with a little of ENV-1 and the keys following, ENV-1 at 1.5/0/9.9/3.9 and ENV-2 at 4.5/0/9.9/5.4, and a second copy layered detuned: here the lower patch in dual, eight cents the other way. From the article: the waves, the detune, the filter, both envelopes and the layer; estimated: the LFO's rate and depth on this panel, the layer's detune. Its chorus is left out.
