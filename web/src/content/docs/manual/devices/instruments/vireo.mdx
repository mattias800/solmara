**An emulation of a digital original,** modelled on the 2000s German virtual-analogue and wavetable synth of trance and dance music.

A wavetable and virtual analogue synth of the early 2000s German kind: two Classic oscillators with sub, third oscillator, noise and ring, two multimode filters with an analogue ladder, four envelopes, three LFOs, a six-slot matrix and unison, wave and HyperSaw oscillator modes and its effects. Its state is the original's 512-byte Single, and its own SysEx patches and banks load. Another track's audio is its external input.

## Presets

30 factory presets come with Vireo. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bass

- **Acrylic Phase Bass**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: the notch bass hard-clipped, its sweep quicker, the mids lifted and a phaser turning through it.
- **Banded Detune Bass**: After a thread on the Dogs On Acid forum: three detuned saws through a band-pass and a notch, a synced LFO turning the cutoff in half notes: a dark, phasing drum-and-bass growl.
- **Glide Acid Line**: After a thread on the maker's forum about acid lines: one saw through the three-pole filter, resonant, accented by velocity, gliding where notes touch, warmed below and echoed in quarters.
- **Rectifier Wah Bass**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: the squelch bass with its filter as a notch that sweeps in slowly, through the rectifier at full: a funky, wah-like growl.
- **Rolling Night Bass**: After a full-on bassline thread on the psynews forum: a saw and a synced saw a fourth below through the four-pole filter, short and tight with a little drive, for rolling sixteenth lines.
- **Squelch Club Bass**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: synced saws over a full sub, the analog boost and punch up, a resonant filter that squelches, its decay shortening the harder it opens.
- **Thunk Saw Bass**: After a thread on the Elektronauts forum: two saws between a shut low-pass and a high-pass, a synced saw LFO knocking the cutoff open in dotted sixteenths and a slow sine wandering under it.

### Brass

- **Hyper Rave Stab**: After Chromatic's drum-and-bass stab walkthrough in Computer Music: saw stacks an octave apart over a low saw, twin unison and hard distortion, a synced LFO moving the mix, all three pitches bent up a fifth by an envelope, through the doppler tape echo.

### FX

- **Contour Dive**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: a one-shot saw LFO dropping the first oscillator from an octave up into pitch at every note, against the steady second: a falling, laser-like dive.
- **Talking Psy Buzz**: After a psytrance effects thread on the maker's forum: a saw whose pitch flicks at each note, filtered by velocity, then the vowel filter wandering on an LFO into a long tape echo.

### Lead

- **Dusty Rave Lead**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: two saws in six-voice unison detuned to the limit, through the low-pass distortion, the wheel cleaning it up.
- **Eight-Voice Unison Lead**: After the First Steps chapter of Howard Scarr's manual for the instrument: two saws stacked eight voices deep, detuned as far as they go and spread across the stereo field, a dotted echo behind, the wheel bringing in vibrato.
- **Formant Pulse Solo**: After Richard Leon's masterclass in Sound On Sound: a thin pulse through two resonant band-passes in parallel, set apart like a voice's formants, gliding, with a vibrato that fades in.
- **Full-Suction Unison Lead**: After the First Steps chapter of Howard Scarr's manual: the eight-voice saw stack with a sub, a little noise and a third saw an octave down, through a short dotted echo; sweep the cutoff while it plays.
- **Narrow Pulse Dive**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: two thin pulses, band-passed and driven hard, a fast LFO shaking their widths under thick chorus; the wheel makes each note dive two octaves.
- **Nine-Saw Lead**: After the First Steps chapter of Howard Scarr's manual: one oscillator as a stack of nine saws spread wide, with its sub, the filter shut and thrown open by its envelope; four unison voices, chorus and a dotted echo spread it.
- **Pressure Glide Lead**: After the First Steps chapter of Howard Scarr's manual: a gliding mono saw lead, the wheel speeding the vibrato and the key's pressure opening the filter.
- **Rectified Solo Lead**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: two saws pushed hard into the saturation, the filter envelope stretching its own attack for a swelling front, bends snapping with no smoothing.
- **Rising Sync Lead**: After Richard Leon's masterclass in Sound On Sound: the second oscillator alone, synced to the first and swept up by the filter envelope at each note, driven a little: a tearing sync lead.
- **Twin-Saw Bright Top Lead**: After a thread on the infekted.org forum about a saw-stack lead on the older model: three detuned voices kept fairly narrow, the second filter unlinked and resonant over the first, a phaser on top.
- **Uplift Stack Lead**: After ADSR Sounds' trance lead walkthrough for the instrument: both oscillators as saw stacks a fifth apart, a third saw and the sub under them, twin unison, a touch of distortion, then echo and a hall.

### Pad

- **Chord Gate Saw Pad**: After Sound Design Tutorials' trance-gate video: both oscillators as saw stacks over a pulse an octave down, the arpeggiator's chord mode chopping held chords in sixteenths with a rest every third step, then chorus, the widener, echo and a hall.
- **Gated Fifths PWM Pad**: After Richard Leon's masterclass in Sound On Sound: two pulses a fifth apart, each gated by its own square LFO pushing its width to silence, one in eighths and one in sixteenths.
- **Matrix Gate Pad**: After a thread on the maker's forum about trance gates: the arpeggiator's steps sent through the matrix to the cutoff, so a pattern of loud and silent steps opens and shuts a detuned saw pad.
- **Quarter Pump Pad**: After a thread on the maker's forum about pumping pads: one LFO, a rising ramp locked to quarter notes, lifts the oscillators' volume from low on every beat, as a kick's side-chain would.

### Pluck

- **Rectified Saw Pluck**: After ADSR Sounds' trance pluck walkthrough, with tips from the maker's forum: a seven-saw stack, the filter snapped open and shut, four unison voices, the rectifier at full, then ping-pong echo and a hall.

### Sequence

- **Dirty Dotted Pluck**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: the same plucked saws arpeggiated into the reverb's feedback mode, a darker, dirtier dotted echo.
- **Dotted Echo Arp**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: a plucked saw pair, brighter up the keyboard, arpeggiated over two octaves into a long dotted-eighth echo whose time wavers.
- **Envelope-LFO Arp Pluck**: After a tips thread on the Elektronauts forum: both filters shut, a one-shot falling LFO plucking them open on every arpeggiator step, through the tape echo and a light phaser.

### Strings

- **Theory Strings**: After Howard Scarr's tutorial for the instrument: two pulses with vibratos of opposite sign, a slow pan, a softer bow the harder you play, and the equaliser lifting lows, mids and highs; no chorus.
