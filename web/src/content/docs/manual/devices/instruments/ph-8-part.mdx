**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on one voice of the early-1980s Japanese analogue rhythm composer.

One channel of the PH-8 alone, for a pad of a drum rack: the tabs on its face choose which, the bass drum, snare, toms and congas, rim shot and claves, clap and maracas, cowbell, cymbal and hats, each keeping its own knobs; any key strikes it.

## Presets

64 factory presets come with PH-8 Part. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Bass drum

- **Click Forward**: The tone up, so the strike's click comes through a mix.
- **Endless Drop**: Decay and sweep at their ends, the tone low: a boom whose pitch is still falling as it fades.
- **Long Boom**: The decay most of the way up: a tail that rings for seconds under a beat, with the click kept back.
- **Machine Kick**: Tone, decay, and sweep at the middle: the machine's own kick, a short snap of pitch and a boom that sighs down to 47 Hz.
- **Round Sub**: The tone at its darkest and a longer decay: all body, no click.
- **Short Thud**: The decay well down: a thud with no tail, for a busy pattern.
- **Sighing Drop**: The sweep past the machine's: the pitch sighs down over most of a second.
- **Tight Punch**: A shorter decay, a quicker sigh, and a little more click.

### Claves

- **Long Claves**: The claves left ringing, their pitch held clear.
- **Low Claves**: The claves tuned down, nearer a woodblock.
- **Machine Claves**: The claves at the middle of every knob: the upper shell ringing high.
- **Woodblock Tick**: The claves tuned up and cut short: a tick for fast patterns.

### Closed hat

- **Dark Hat**: The closed hat with its band and high-pass moved down: duller, nearer a shaker.
- **Loose Hat**: The closed hat let ring a little, between closed and open.
- **Machine Closed Hat**: The closed hat as the machine has it: a short, bright tick of six square waves.
- **Sizzle Hat**: The closed hat with its band and high-pass moved up: thin and bright.
- **Tight Hat**: The closed hat cut to a click, for fast patterns.

### Cowbell

- **Agogo**: The cowbell tuned up and shortened, nearer an agogo bell.
- **Choked Bell**: The cowbell at its shortest: a clank.
- **Clank**: The cowbell at the top of its tune and cut short.
- **Deep Bell**: The cowbell an octave down, ringing long.
- **High Cowbell**: The cowbell tuned up a little.
- **Low Cowbell**: The cowbell tuned down a little.
- **Machine Cowbell**: The cowbell as the machine has it: two square waves, a sharp start and a long ring.
- **Short Cowbell**: The cowbell cut short.

### Cymbal

- **Bright Cymbal**: The cymbal with its bright band turned up.
- **Dark Cymbal**: The cymbal with its bright band turned down: all ring and little splash.
- **Long Crash**: The cymbal at its longest and a little brighter.
- **Low Cymbal**: The cymbal with its bands at their lowest, ringing long.
- **Machine Cymbal**: The cymbal as the machine has it: a bright splash that rings on lower.
- **Short Cymbal**: The cymbal at its shortest.
- **Splash**: The cymbal brightened, raised, and cut short.
- **Wash**: The cymbal darkened and lowered, ringing long, nearer a ride's wash.

### Hand clap

- **Bright Clap**: The clap with its band moved up: sharper and nearer.
- **Dark Clap**: The clap with its band moved down: thicker and further off.
- **Machine Hand Clap**: The hand clap at the middle of its tone: three quick slaps and the reverberation after.
- **Thin Clap**: The clap at the top of its tone, for a light backbeat.

### High conga

- **Bongo Tap**: The high conga tuned up and cut short.
- **High Conga**: The high conga.

### High tom

- **Machine High Tom**: The high tom at the middle of every knob.

### Low conga

- **Low Conga**: The low conga: the low tom's circuit with its capacitors switched out, higher and without the noise.

### Low tom

- **Deep Floor Tom**: The low tom tuned down, its decay drawn out, and more of its tail.
- **Machine Low Tom**: The low tom at the middle of every knob, with its tail of pink noise.

### Maracas

- **Bright Shaker**: The maracas brightened to an airy hiss.
- **Machine Maracas**: The maracas at the middle of their tone: a short hiss that swells and stops.
- **Muffled Maracas**: The maracas at the bottom of their tone.
- **Soft Shaker**: The maracas darkened, for a shaker that sits back.

### Mid conga

- **Mid Conga**: The mid conga.

### Mid tom

- **Machine Mid Tom**: The mid tom at the middle of every knob.

### Open hat

- **Long Open Hat**: The open hat at its longest, a wash under the beat.
- **Machine Open Hat**: The open hat as the machine has it: the same metal, left to ring.
- **Short Open Hat**: The open hat shortened to a quick hiss.

### Rim shot

- **High Rim**: The rim shot tuned to its top.
- **Machine Rim Shot**: The rim shot at the middle of every knob: both shells clipped into a bright knock.
- **Ringing Rim**: The rim shot let ring, its lower shell sounding on under the knock.
- **Tight Rim**: The rim shot cut short, all knock and no ring.

### Snare drum

- **Crack**: The tone toward the upper shell and more snappy: a short, bright crack.
- **Fat Body**: The tone toward the lower shell and the snappy down: more drum than snares.
- **Long Hiss**: Snappy and decay at their tops, the tone bright: a wash of noise past the machine's.
- **Machine Snare**: Tone, snappy, and decay at the middle: the machine's own snare, two shells and a burst of noise.
- **Ringing Shells**: The snappy all but off: the two shells alone, a pitched knock.
- **Soft Brush**: The snappy low and the decay long: a quiet hiss for ballads.
- **Tight Snap**: The decay down: a short snap for a busy pattern.
- **Wire Rattle**: Snappy near its top and the decay drawn out: the snares ring on after the shells.
