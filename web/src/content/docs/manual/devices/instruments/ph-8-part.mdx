**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on one voice of the early-1980s Japanese analogue rhythm composer.

One channel of the PH-8 alone, for a pad of a drum rack: the tabs on its face choose which, the bass drum, snare, toms and congas, rim shot and claves, clap and maracas, cowbell, cymbal and hats, each keeping its own knobs; any key strikes it.

## Appendix: the factory presets

PH-8 Part comes with 64 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass drum:** Click Forward, Endless Drop, Long Boom, Machine Kick, Round Sub, Short Thud, Sighing Drop, Tight Punch.
- **Claves:** Long Claves, Low Claves, Machine Claves, Woodblock Tick.
- **Closed hat:** Dark Hat, Loose Hat, Machine Closed Hat, Sizzle Hat, Tight Hat.
- **Cowbell:** Agogo, Choked Bell, Clank, Deep Bell, High Cowbell, Low Cowbell, Machine Cowbell, Short Cowbell.
- **Cymbal:** Bright Cymbal, Dark Cymbal, Long Crash, Low Cymbal, Machine Cymbal, Short Cymbal, Splash, Wash.
- **Hand clap:** Bright Clap, Dark Clap, Machine Hand Clap, Thin Clap.
- **High conga:** Bongo Tap, High Conga.
- **High tom:** Machine High Tom.
- **Low conga:** Low Conga.
- **Low tom:** Deep Floor Tom, Machine Low Tom.
- **Maracas:** Bright Shaker, Machine Maracas, Muffled Maracas, Soft Shaker.
- **Mid conga:** Mid Conga.
- **Mid tom:** Machine Mid Tom.
- **Open hat:** Long Open Hat, Machine Open Hat, Short Open Hat.
- **Rim shot:** High Rim, Machine Rim Shot, Ringing Rim, Tight Rim.
- **Snare drum:** Crack, Fat Body, Long Hiss, Machine Snare, Ringing Shells, Soft Brush, Tight Snap, Wire Rattle.
