**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1970s German electric clavichord of funk.

A modelled electric clavichord of the 1960s and 1970s, built from its physics and held to a reference by measurement: a rubber tangent strikes each steel string and holds it against an anvil while the key is down, the harder the brighter, and the string rings between the tangent and the bridge, a little stiff, its upper partials sharp. Let go, the string is its whole length in the yarn, and sounds its release's thunk a little lower as the yarn takes it down. Two electromagnetic pickups under the strings and their two rocker switches, one pickup or the other or both in and out of phase; the four tone switches, filters that add; the mute slider. 24 strings sound at once.
