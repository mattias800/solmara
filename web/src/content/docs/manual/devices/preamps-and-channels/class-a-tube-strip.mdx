**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the late-1990s American class-A valve channel strip.

A modeled class-A valve channel strip of the late 1990s: a line or microphone input and a high-pass, a valve preamplifier in a loop of heavy feedback with a High Gain switch, an optical compressor whose lamp lights a photoresistor against a ratio pot, its attack and release on two pots and a faster attack switch, and a four-band discrete equalizer (a bass and a treble shelf, two sweeping mids with x10 and Q switches) that can go ahead of the compressor or into its sidechain; then the output. At two or four times the sample rate, as late at both.
