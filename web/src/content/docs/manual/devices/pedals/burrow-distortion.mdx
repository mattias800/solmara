**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the late-1970s American op-amp distortion pedal.

An op-amp distortion for guitar: a slow op-amp whose gain the distortion knob raises, its bandwidth narrowing as it does, clipping into two diodes to ground, then a filter knob that darkens it. From a gritty overdrive to a thick, saturated distortion. Distortion, filter, volume, and an output; light or full quality. Modeled on the American op-amp distortion pedal of the late 1970s.
