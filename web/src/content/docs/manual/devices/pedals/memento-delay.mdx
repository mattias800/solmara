**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the late-1970s American bucket-brigade delay pedal with its chorus and vibrato.

A modeled bucket-brigade delay of the pedal kind, for guitars, keys, and synths: one long chain of capacitors that a clock passes the signal along, between a compressor and an expander. The delay is the line's length over the clock's rate, so the longer the delay, the slower the clock and the darker each repeat; the compander keeps the line's hiss down and lets the repeats swell and smear. Delay, feedback up to where the repeats grow by themselves, blend, the level into the line, which clips it when turned up, and the echo's level; a chorus or vibrato that swings the clock, which bends every repeat in pitch; and a standard or a long line, some 43 to 410 ms or 112 ms to 1.1 s. Modeled on the large bucket-brigade echo pedal of the late 1970s with chorus and vibrato.

## Appendix: the factory presets

Memento Delay comes with 6 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).
