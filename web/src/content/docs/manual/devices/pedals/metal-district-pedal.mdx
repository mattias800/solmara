**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the early-1990s Japanese high-gain metal pedal with its three-band EQ.

A modeled high-gain distortion pedal with an equalizer of its own: a resonant stage lifts the upper middle round 1 kHz before a gain stage and two diodes to ground clip it hard, a second resonant stage lifts the lows round 100 Hz and the top round 5 kHz after, and then an active equalizer with a high, a low, and a middle of 15 dB either way whose frequency is swept from some 180 Hz to 6 kHz. Dist, level, high, low, middle, and middle frequency, a hiss knob for the circuit's own noise, and a quality switch for how far above the sample rate the circuits are solved. It is made to be played into a clean amplifier, and with its middle cut it is the scooped sound of 1990s metal. Modeled on the orange and black distortion pedal of the early 1990s, named for a zone of metal, with its two concentric equalizer knobs.
