A MIDI device: lets through the notes within a range of keys and of velocities, or outside it, and moves, fixes or scales their pitch, octave, velocity on a curve, and length, limits how many sound at once, and holds them.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Note Filter. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Even Percussion**: Every note at a steady velocity and a short trigger length.
- **Four Voice Chords**: Four voices, retaining the most recently played chord tones.
- **Left Hand Bass**: Only the lower keyboard, moved down an octave and kept monophonic.
- **Low Octave Split**: The lower hand drops an octave while the upper hand passes through.
- **Quiet Keys Only**: Keep softly played notes and give them a useful dynamic range.
- **Right Hand Bell**: Only middle C and above, lifted an octave.
- **Soft Electric Keys**: A gentle velocity curve that keeps the loudest keys restrained.
- **Staccato Eighths**: Long notes end after an eighth; short notes keep their articulation.
