A MIDI device: moves the track's notes by steps of a scale, by semitones, and by octaves before the instrument plays them, in the project's scale or one of its own.

## Appendix: every control

Each of the device's controls by the name automation and the inspector give it, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scale steps | -14 to 14 | 0 |
| Semitones | -24 st to 24 st | 0 st |
| Octaves | -4 oct to 4 oct | 0 oct |
| Scale | 15 choices: Project, Major, Minor, Dorian, … | Project |
| Root | 12 choices: C, C#, D, D#, … | C |
