A MIDI device: lets notes through by a sequence of yes and no, or by chance, and by chance sets or moves their pitch, velocity and length within ranges, each with a chance of its own or one for all.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Note Chance. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Chord Conversation**: Alternate whole held chords rather than their individual notes.
- **Every Other Key**: Alternate played notes, keeping pitch and touch unchanged.
- **Human Touch**: Small velocity changes on every key.
- **Linked Surprises**: Occasional higher, softer, shorter notes; all three changes happen together.
- **Octave Sparks**: One note in four jumps up an octave.
- **Short or Long**: Notes choose between a sixteenth and a quarter.
- **Sparse Reply**: About two thirds of the notes pass, with a little variation of touch.
- **Three Note Answer**: Three notes through, then one rest.
