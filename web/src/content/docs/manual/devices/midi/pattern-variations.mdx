A MIDI device: plays a polyphonic pattern of steps, and makes variations of it at a click: notes moved by octaves and fifths or further, swapped, thinned or filled, lengthened, and accented. Eight patterns, transposed from the keys.

[Pattern Variations on the website](/solmara/write/#pattern-variations)

## Appendix: the factory presets

Pattern Variations comes with 8 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

Broken Fifths, Held Minor Phrase, Legato Acid Steps, Minor Ninth Pulses, Quiet Sixteenths, Root and Reply, Skewed Seven, Soft Chord Ostinato.

## Appendix: every control

Each of the device's controls by the name automation and the inspector give it, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Playback mode | SEQ, SEQ+KEY, KEY | SEQ |
| Transpose | Off, On | Off |
| Scale velocity | Off, On | Off |
| Pattern | 1 to 8 | 1 |
| Play mutation | Off, On | Off |
| Local shuffle | 50% to 75% | 66% |
| Octave+Pitch | 0% to 100% | 30% |
| Swap | 0% to 100% | 20% |
| Density | 0% to 100% | 30% |
| Note length | 0% to 100% | 20% |
| Velocity (accent) | 0% to 100% | 30% |
| Shuffle source | Local, Global | Local |
