A MIDI device: repeats each note, as a delay repeats sound, up to sixteen times, each repeat a step later, moved in pitch from the one before and louder or softer. With no time between them the repeats stack into chords.

## Presets

10 factory presets come with Note Repeater. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Dotted Answers**: Two dotted-eighth answers, each a fifth higher.
- **Falling Octaves**: Three eighth-note echoes falling an octave each time.
- **Major Seventh**: A close major seventh on every key.
- **Minor Ninth**: A parallel minor ninth: root, minor third, fifth, seventh and ninth.
- **Open Fifths**: Root, fifth and octave, leaving room for a melody's thirds.
- **Quiet Sixteenths**: Four soft sixteenth echoes behind short keys.
- **Rising Minor Strum**: A minor seventh strummed gently from low to high.
- **Suspended Seconds**: A stack of seconds and fifths, without a third.
- **Suspended Strum**: An open suspended chord spread across a short strum.
- **Triplet Climb**: Triplet echoes climbing in minor thirds.

## Controls

Each of the device's controls, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tempo sync | Off, On | On |
| Step | 16 choices: 1/128, 1/64T, 1/64, 1/32T, … | 1/8 |
| Step time | 0 ms to 1000 ms | 250 ms |
| Repeats | 1 to 16 | 3 |
| Velocity | 10% to 200% | 70% |
| Pitch | -12 st to 12 st | 0 st |
