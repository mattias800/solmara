A MIDI device: repeats each note, as a delay repeats sound, up to sixteen times, each repeat a step later, moved in pitch from the one before and louder or softer. With no time between them the repeats stack into chords.

[Note Repeater on the website](/solmara/write/#note-repeater)

## Appendix: the factory presets

Note Repeater comes with 10 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

Dotted Answers, Falling Octaves, Major Seventh, Minor Ninth, Open Fifths, Quiet Sixteenths, Rising Minor Strum, Suspended Seconds, Suspended Strum, Triplet Climb.

## Appendix: every control

Each of the device's controls by the name automation and the inspector give it, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tempo sync | Off, On | On |
| Step | 16 choices: 1/128, 1/64T, 1/64, 1/32T, … | 1/8 |
| Step time | 0 ms to 1000 ms | 250 ms |
| Repeats | 1 to 16 | 3 |
| Velocity | 10% to 200% | 70% |
| Pitch | -12 st to 12 st | 0 st |
