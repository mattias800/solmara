A MIDI device: makes a bass line of an on-beat phrase and an off-beat one, chosen from busy to sparse and varied through the pattern, its intervals stretched and made minor, transposed from the keys or played as written, with eight patterns.

[Bassline Maker on the website](/solmara/write/#bassline-maker)

## Appendix: the factory presets

Bassline Maker comes with 8 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

Dub Roots, Funk Thread, Key Played Bass, Longer Conversation, Minor Garage, Octave Floor, Root Pocket, Tied Corners.
