A MIDI device: sixteen chords on pads, from sets in many styles or written by hand, each lit by how well it would follow the last, played from the keys or by a progression of up to sixteen bars, with velocity, humanizing, a bass taken away or a root added, and eight patterns.

[Chord Pads on the website](/solmara/write/#chord-pads)

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Chord Pads. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **House Balcony**: Four house chords, ready for a short piano sound.
- **Jazz After Hours**: A compact four-bar jazz accompaniment.
- **Lydian Morning**: Bright, suspended harmony for a wide pad.
- **Minor Evening**: A minor-key chord bed for slow melodies.
- **Nordic Air**: A modal progression with room above its bass.
- **Open Pop**: A four-bar major-key pad progression.
- **Soul Lanterns**: Warm extended chords for electric keys.
- **Still Water Pads**: A quiet progression of spacious ambient chords.
