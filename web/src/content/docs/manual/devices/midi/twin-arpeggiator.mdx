A MIDI device: two arpeggiators side by side, each over the held notes in its own range of keys, up, down, up and down or at random over one to four octaves, or as a pattern of up to four notes a step, with a velocity lane, a gate, and a hold. Notes outside both ranges pass through.

[Twin Arpeggiator on the website](/solmara/write/#twin-arpeggiator)

## Appendix: the factory presets

Twin Arpeggiator comes with 8 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

Crossed Diagonals, Falling Eighths, Octave Weave, Porcelain Steps, Pulsing Chord, Shuffled Lanterns, Two Hands, Uneven Walk.
