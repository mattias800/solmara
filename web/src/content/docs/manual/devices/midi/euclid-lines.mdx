A MIDI device: four lines that each play a Euclidean rhythm at a rate of their own, with pitches, velocities and lengths varied by chance about a base key, kept to a scale, frozen in a zone, and joined as chords or as one line. It plays with the song, or while a key is held, about that key.

[Euclid Lines on the website](/solmara/write/#euclid-lines)

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Euclid Lines. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass and Flecks**: A low pulse under a quieter high line.
- **Dorian Stream**: A Dorian line with small variations of touch and length.
- **Four Line Canopy**: Four sparse lines form an evolving minor-key canopy.
- **Frozen Bells**: Two octaves of pentatonic bells, their first cycle frozen.
- **Held Key Clouds**: A held key starts a gentle, shifting minor-pentatonic cloud.
- **Low Note Garden**: Two lines compete for one voice; the lower notes win.
- **Shuffled Copper**: A shuffling line about the base key, with a little extra density.
- **Three Against Four**: Two interlocking Euclidean lines, a fifth apart.
