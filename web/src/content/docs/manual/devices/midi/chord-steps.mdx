A MIDI device: a polyphonic step sequencer in a scale, whose notes can be boxes that play chords, repeats, arpeggios or strums, with eight patterns of four variations each that switch by themselves, a velocity lane, and transposition from the keys.

[Chord Steps on the website](/solmara/write/#chord-steps)

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Chord Steps. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Dorian Chord Walk**: A complete pattern of dorian chord walk.
- **Four Bar Arcs**: A complete pattern of four bar arcs.
- **Held Key Jet-8000**: A complete pattern of held key jet.
- **Legato Footsteps**: A complete pattern of legato footsteps.
- **Minor Bell Steps**: A complete pattern of minor bell steps.
- **Offbeat Stabs**: A complete pattern of offbeat stabs.
- **Repeated Fifths**: A complete pattern of repeated fifths.
- **Wide Lydian Strums**: A complete pattern of wide lydian strums.

## Controls

Each of the device's controls, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play mode | Seq, Key | Seq |
| MIDI transpose | Off, On, On latch | Off |
| Pattern | 1 to 8 | 1 |
| Variation | I, II, III, IV | I |
| Auto switch | Off, Next, Random, I-II, III-IV, I-I-I-II | Off |
| Shuffle | 50% to 75% | 66% |
| Shuffle source | Local, Global | Local |
