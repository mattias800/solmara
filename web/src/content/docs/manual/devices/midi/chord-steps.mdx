A MIDI device: a polyphonic step sequencer in a scale, whose notes can be boxes that play chords, repeats, arpeggios or strums, with eight patterns of four variations each that switch by themselves, a velocity lane, and transposition from the keys.

## Appendix: the factory presets

Chord Steps comes with 8 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

Dorian Chord Walk, Four Bar Arcs, Held Key Jet-8000, Legato Footsteps, Minor Bell Steps, Offbeat Stabs, Repeated Fifths, Wide Lydian Strums.

## Appendix: every control

Each of the device's controls by the name automation and the inspector give it, with the values it can take and the one it starts at.

| Control | Range | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play mode | Seq, Key | Seq |
| MIDI transpose | Off, On, On latch | Off |
| Pattern | 1 to 8 | 1 |
| Variation | I, II, III, IV | I |
| Auto switch | Off, Next, Random, I-II, III-IV, I-I-I-II | Off |
| Shuffle | 50% to 75% | 66% |
| Shuffle source | Local, Global | Local |
