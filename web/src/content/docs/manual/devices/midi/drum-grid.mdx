A MIDI device: plays eight channels of sixteen steps as the song plays, each a key of the instrument such as a drum rack's pad, with eight patterns to choose between. Each channel has its own length, speed, direction and slide, and each step a velocity, repeats, triplets or a flam, and a probability. The track's own notes pass through.

## Appendix: the factory presets

Drum Grid comes with 8 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

Clave Conversation, Electro Corners, Four on the Floor, Garage Skips, Half Time Dust, Late Night Break, Pocket Backbeat, Soft Shuffle.
