A MIDI device: plays eight channels of sixteen steps as the song plays, each a key of the instrument such as a drum rack's pad, with eight patterns to choose between. Each channel has its own length, speed, direction and slide, and each step a velocity, repeats, triplets or a flam, and a probability. The track's own notes pass through.

## Presets

8 factory presets come with Drum Grid. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Clave Conversation**: A clave-shaped rim against a sparse kick and tom reply.
- **Electro Corners**: An angular electro beat with a rim answering the snare.
- **Four on the Floor**: A steady kick, claps on two and four and offbeat open hats.
- **Garage Skips**: A shuffled two-step kick and a backbeat with a hat answer.
- **Half Time Dust**: A half-time snare, sparse kick and rolling hats.
- **Late Night Break**: A syncopated break with soft snare ghosts.
- **Pocket Backbeat**: A restrained rock pocket with a pickup into the fourth beat.
- **Soft Shuffle**: A relaxed shuffle with soft notes between the backbeats.
