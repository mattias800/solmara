**A design of Solmara's own.**

A chromatic tuner for guitar, bass, and anything that holds a note: it shows the note it hears and how many cents sharp or flat, against a reference for A4, and passes the audio as it came or mutes it while tuning, as a tuner pedal does. It reads a steady string to within half a cent from a dropped low D up. Agents read it with the tuner command.

## Presets

3 factory presets come with Tuner. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **A at 432**: A at 432 Hz, for playing along with a recording or an instrument tuned so.
- **Tune Live**: The audio passes as it came, so the tuner can stay on while playing.
- **Tune Silent**: Muted while tuning, against A at 440 Hz, as a tuner pedal on the board.
