**An emulation of a digital original,** modelled on the 1990s pitch-shifting expression pedal.

A pitch-shift pedal for guitar: an expression pedal sweeps the shift between a heel and a toe interval, up to two octaves either way, for octave leads, dive bombs, harmonies and detuned doubling, and drops a tuning without retuning. Polyphonic and 10 ms late whatever the interval, or 16 ms to join low chords; a classic character glitches as the early digital pedals did. Pedal, heel, toe, mix, character, glide, an octave-down and a semitone-up layer, an output, and the latency.

## Presets

6 factory presets come with Whiny Shifter. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Detune Doubler**: Twelve cents up mixed with the dry signal at the heel and the toe alike: a thickening, chorus-like double.
- **Dive Bomb**: Heel at unison, toe two octaves down, with a little glide: stomp it for a dive like a tremolo arm's.
- **Fifth Harmony**: A fifth above, mixed with the dry signal: parallel harmony from the toe, unison at the heel.
- **Octave Down**: Heel at unison, toe an octave down: a bass line under a riff, or a low tuning without retuning.
- **Octave Up Lead**: Heel at unison, toe an octave up: rock the pedal for the classic screaming octave sweep.
- **Two Octaves Up**: Heel at unison, toe two octaves up, for the shrieking top of a solo.
