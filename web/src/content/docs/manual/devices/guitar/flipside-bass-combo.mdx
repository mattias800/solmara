**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1960s American flip-top valve bass combo of the studios.

A modelled 1960s flip-top bass combo with its sealed 1x15: two channels, the 1964 preamp and the 1966 one, each two valve stages with a volume and a passive bass and treble, into a small push-pull pair of beam tetrodes on a valve rectifier that sags, with no master. Volume, bass, and treble, the channel, the jack, the bias of either year, and the cabinet on or off. Round and soft, the studio and soul bass sound: modelled on the flip-top combo of the 1960s.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Flipside Bass Combo. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bright Sixty-Six**: The 1966 channel, thinner in the lows and brighter round 2 kHz: a clearer note for a pick or a busy line.
- **Pushed Combo**: The volume up past 80: the small power stage squashes and sags on the low notes, a soft, warm grind.
- **Quiet Jack**: The second, quieter jack with the volume up: clean headroom for a hot active bass.
- **Soul Thump**: The bass up and the treble down, a little louder: a thick, short thump for fingers and flatwounds.
- **Studio Round**: The 1964 channel with the volume low and the knobs near noon: round and warm, the studio bass of the 1960s.
