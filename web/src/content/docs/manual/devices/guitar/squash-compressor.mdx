**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1970s American OTA compressor pedal.

A compressor pedal for guitar: a transconductance gain cell whose gain a detector of its own output pulls down, fast to clamp and slow to let go. It evens out picking, lifts a fading note into a long sustain, and squashes the attack of a clean funk or country part. Sustain, volume, and an output. Modelled on the orange and red compressor pedals of the 1970s.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Squash Compressor. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Before the Fuzz**: Squashed hard and a little loud, to feed a fuzz or a drive after it a steady level.
- **Country Squash**: The sustain past half: every pick evened out, the attacks flattened, for clean chicken picking.
- **Endless Sustain**: Full sustain: a held note lifted as it fades, for clean leads and swells.
- **Funk Rhythm**: A moderate squash that keeps sixteenth-note strums level and snappy.
- **Gentle Leveller**: The sustain low: a light hand on the loudest notes, always on.
