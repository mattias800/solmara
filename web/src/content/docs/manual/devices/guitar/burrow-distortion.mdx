**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1970s American op-amp distortion pedal.

An op-amp distortion for guitar: a slow op-amp whose gain the distortion knob raises, its bandwidth narrowing as it does, clipping into two diodes to ground, then a filter knob that darkens it. From a gritty overdrive to a thick, saturated distortion. Distortion, filter, volume, and an output; light or full quality. Modelled on the American op-amp distortion pedal of the late 1970s.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Burrow Distortion. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Classic Crunch**: The distortion half way, the filter a little closed: the gritty crunch of a late-seventies rock rhythm.
- **Dark Lead**: Full distortion with the filter well closed: a smooth, dark lead that sustains.
- **Edge of Breakup**: The distortion low: a mild, open overdrive that pushes an amplifier.
- **Fuzzed Bass**: On a bass at full quality: grit on the notes, the filter keeping the top civil.
- **Grunge Wall**: Most of the distortion, the filter open: thick, raw and loud.
