**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1960s American 300-watt valve bass head and its cabinet of eight tens.

A modelled all-valve bass head of 300 watts with its 8x10 cabinet, both its channels: in each an input valve, the ultra low switch into the volume with the ultra high switch's capacitor round it, a second valve and a passive bass and treble stack, the first with a middle of three frequencies and a bass cut; and a power stage of six output valves on a stiff supply, with no master, so the volume drives the power stage too. Volume, bass, middle, and treble, the channel, ultra high, ultra low, the normal or bright jack, and the cabinet on or off. Modelled on the 1969 rock bass head and its fridge-sized cabinet, built from its published drawing.

## Presets

7 factory presets come with Icebox Bass Stack. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Flat Out**: The volume near its top: the six output valves clip, thick and compressed, under a preamp that is still nearly clean.
- **Mid Punch**: The first channel with its middle lifted at 800 Hz and the volume past half way: the notes push through a loud band, the stack's punchy rock sound.
- **Pick Clank**: Ultra high on and the treble up at a middling volume: the pick's attack and the strings' clank on top of the growl, for punk and metal.
- **Rock Growl**: The volume past half way and the stack a little up at both ends: a played bass growls on the hard notes and stays round on the soft ones, the classic rock bass sound.
- **Round Fingers**: The volume low, the bass up and the treble down: a warm, round sound for fingers that stays clean.
- **Scooped Growl**: The first channel with its middle cut low and the bass and treble up, driven: a deep, wide growl under heavy guitars.
- **Ultra Low Deep**: Ultra low on, the lows straight through and the rest some 8 dB down, with the treble low: deep and dark for slow lines and reggae.
