**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1960s American one-transistor clean boost.

A modelled one-transistor clean boost, for guitar into an amplifier: a silicon transistor in common emitter with an unbypassed emitter resistor and a volume pot after it, the circuit most clean boosts are built on. It lifts a guitar by up to some 26 dB, cleanly at a guitar's level; a hot input flattens it, one side before the other, which pushes an amplifier's input into breaking up. One knob, the boost, and an output. Modelled on the small linear booster of the late 1960s.

## Presets

4 factory presets come with Lift Pedal. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Always On**: A few decibels over unity, left on: a little more level and sparkle into the amplifier.
- **Hot Stage**: Full up with the output turned down: the transistor's own lopsided clipping, heard at a sensible level.
- **Push the Amp**: Full up: the stage starts to flatten on hard notes, and the amplifier after it breaks up sooner.
- **Solo Lift**: Some 12 dB up for a solo, still clean on a guitar played normally.
