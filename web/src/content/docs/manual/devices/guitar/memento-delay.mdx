**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1970s American bucket-brigade delay pedal with its chorus and vibrato.

A modelled bucket-brigade delay of the pedal kind, for guitars, keys, and synths: one long chain of capacitors that a clock passes the signal along, between a compressor and an expander. The delay is the line's length over the clock's rate, so the longer the delay, the slower the clock and the darker each repeat; the compander keeps the line's hiss down and lets the repeats swell and smear. Delay, feedback up to where the repeats grow by themselves, blend, the level into the line, which clips it when turned up, and the echo's level; a chorus or vibrato that swings the clock, which bends every repeat in pitch; and a standard or a long line, some 43 to 410 ms or 112 ms to 1.1 s. Modelled on the large bucket-brigade echo pedal of the late 1970s with chorus and vibrato.

## Presets

6 factory presets come with Memento Delay. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

### Delay

- **Chorused Echo**: A medium echo with the chorus swinging the clock, so every repeat wavers in pitch: the pedal's best-known sound.
- **Dark Repeats**: Some 300 ms and several repeats, each darker than the last, under a lead.
- **Long Wash**: The long line at some 700 ms, dark and smeared, with a slow chorus: a wash behind chords.
- **Self-Oscillation**: The feedback past where the repeats die away: they grow until the line clips them, and the delay knob bends them in pitch as it turns.
- **Slapback**: The shortest delay, one repeat and a little more, under the dry signal: the slap of the rockabilly records.

### Modulation

- **Vibrato**: The echo alone at the shortest delay, the vibrato fast and deep: the pitch wobbles and nothing repeats.
