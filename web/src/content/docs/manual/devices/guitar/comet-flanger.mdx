**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the bucket-brigade flanger pedals of the 1970s.

A modelled bucket-brigade flanger, for guitars, keys, drums, and whole mixes: one short chain of capacitors that a clock passes the signal along, between a compressor and an expander, mixed with the dry signal, which cuts a comb of notches that sweeps as the clock does. Manual sets the delay, from a fraction of a millisecond to some ten; width how far a slow oscillator sweeps it from there; speed how fast, from a sweep over a minute long to five a second; regeneration feeds the wet path back for a ringing, metallic jet; mix sets the two paths, deepest at the middle. Modelled on the rack and pedal flangers of the late 1970s and early 1980s.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Comet Flanger. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Drum Whoosh**: The shortest delays and back, with a little regeneration, for a drum bus that whooshes.
- **Fast Warble**: A quick, shallow sweep: a warble that sits between a flanger and a vibrato.
- **Gentle Swirl**: A narrow, slow sweep at the long end with no regeneration, nearer a chorus than a jet.
- **Jet Sweep**: The whole sweep, slowly, with the regeneration up: the jet plane taking off and landing.
- **Metal Ring**: Held still with the regeneration full: a fixed comb that rings like a pipe, on drums or a riff.
