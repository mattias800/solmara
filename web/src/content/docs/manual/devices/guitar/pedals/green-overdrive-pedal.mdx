**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1970s Japanese green overdrive pedal.

A modelled overdrive pedal that lifts the middle of a guitar's band: an operational amplifier with two diodes back to back in its feedback, which clips softly and only what lies above the lows, so the low end passes clean and stays tight, then a tone control and a level. Drive, tone, and level, with a quality switch for how far above the sample rate the circuits are solved. With the drive low and the level high it is the boost put in front of a high-gain amplifier to tighten rhythm playing. Modelled on the small green overdrive pedal of the late 1970s, named for making a valve amplifier scream, the usual pedal before a high-gain head.
