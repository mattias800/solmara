**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on a modern three-channel high-gain valve head with a temper switch.

A modelled three-channel high-gain valve head with a temper switch, and a cabinet that can be switched off: an input valve, the temper's voicing and the gain pot, a second valve, a third on the high and insane channels, a clipping stage that clips one side of the wave first, a fourth stage into a cathode follower, a bass, middle, and treble stack after the clipping, the master, and the power stage's loop with its depth and presence. Clean, high, and insane channels; calm, fierce, and rage tempers, each a set of the circuit's values fitted to a reference. It holds the audio back by 38 frames.

## Presets

6 factory presets come with Berserk Amp. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Clean Calm**: The clean channel at calm, the gain past its middle: clean and bright, breaking up only when played hard.
- **Clean Rage Crunch**: The clean channel at rage: the gain of a crunch amp, and the top lifted before it.
- **Fierce Rhythm**: The high channel at fierce, the depth held back: the lows cut before the gain, for tight palm mutes on a low-tuned guitar.
- **High Calm**: The high channel at calm with the gain and every tone knob at noon: thick and dark, the lows kept, saturated from a quarter of the gain.
- **Insane Chug**: The insane channel at fierce with the gain low: one more stage and the lows cut before it, for fast, muted chugs.
- **Rage Lead**: The high channel at rage, the gain past its middle and the middle up: the most gain of the high channel, for long notes.
