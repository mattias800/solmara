A modelled one-transistor clean boost, for guitar into an amplifier: a silicon transistor in common emitter with an unbypassed emitter resistor and a volume pot after it, the circuit most clean boosts are built on. It lifts a guitar by up to some 26 dB, cleanly at a guitar's level; a hot input flattens it, one side before the other, which pushes an amplifier's input into breaking up. One knob, the boost, and an output. Modelled on the small linear booster of the late 1960s.
