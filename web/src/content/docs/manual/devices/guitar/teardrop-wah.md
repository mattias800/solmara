**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1960s inductor wah pedal.

An inductor wah pedal for guitar, keys and bass: a resonant peak that the pedal sweeps from some 480 Hz at the heel to 2 kHz at the toe, the lows held under it. The pedal is a parameter to automate or to map a MIDI expression pedal to; or an envelope follower or an oscillator moves it, as an auto-wah. Pedal, mode, depth, rate, sensitivity, and an output. Modelled on the American wah of the late 1960s.
