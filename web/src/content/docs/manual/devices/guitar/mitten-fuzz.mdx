**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1970s American four-stage sustaining fuzz.

A four-stage sustaining fuzz for guitar and bass: a booster into two transistor stages that each clip softly and evenly, a tone stack that blends a low-pass and a high-pass with a scooped middle between them, and a recovery stage. A thick, smooth wall of fuzz with a long, singing sustain. Sustain, tone, volume, and an output; light or full quality. Modelled on the big American fuzz of the early 1970s.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Mitten Fuzz. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bright Fuzz**: The tone well over to the treble: a cutting fuzz that sits on top of a band.
- **Doom Bass**: The tone dark, for bass or tuned-down riffs: all low end and grind, at full quality.
- **Gritty Rhythm**: The sustain low: a rough, open fuzz that keeps chords apart.
- **Singing Lead**: Full sustain, the tone a little bright: a note that sings and hangs.
- **Wall of Fuzz**: The sustain up and the tone at noon: the thick, smooth wall with the scooped middle.
