A two-transistor fuzz for guitar, germanium or silicon: two transistors in common emitter fed back on each other, clipping hard and lopsided into a thick, buzzing sustain that cleans up as the guitar is turned down. Germanium is rounder and darker with a thinner bass at full fuzz; silicon has more gain and a brighter, more even edge. Fuzz, volume, the transistors, and an output; light or full quality. Modelled on the round fuzz pedal of the late 1960s and fitted to a model of it.
