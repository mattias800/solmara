**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1990s American hybrid bass head: a valve preamp into a solid-state power amp.

A modelled hybrid bass head with its cabinet: a valve preamp with a gain, bright, ultra high and ultra low, an active bass, a middle of five frequencies and a treble, a valve drive stage of its own, a nine-band graphic EQ, and a solid-state power amp behind a master. Punchy and tight: modelled on the rack-era hybrid bass head.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Halfglow Bass Head. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Low Mid Growl**: The middle up at 550 Hz and the gain past half way: a growling low middle that cuts through heavy guitars.
- **Modern Smile**: The graphic EQ in, the lows and highs up and the middle down, with bright on: a wide, hi-fi modern sound.
- **Tight Punch**: The gain at 40 with the middle a little up at 1 kHz: a punchy, even note that sits under a band.
- **Tube Grind**: The gain high and the tube gain down: the valve drive stage grinds and grows its second harmonic.
- **Ultra Low Slap**: Ultra low and ultra high on, the treble up: the scooped, bright sound for slap and pop.
