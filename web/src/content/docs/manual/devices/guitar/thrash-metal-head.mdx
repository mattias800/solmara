**A design of Solmara's own.**

A modelled British master-volume guitar head of the early 1980s as a thrash player had it built for him, without a cabinet: a boost before the valves, a voiced equaliser with its own gain (Assault), and an expander gate that follows the guitar's level, both in while the boost is on; then an input valve and the preamp volume, a cold-biased second stage, a third stage into a cathode follower, a passive tone stack, a master volume, and four European output pentodes in push-pull. Preamp volume, master volume, bass, middle, treble, presence, the boost, assault, and the gate's threshold. It wants a cabinet after it, the speaker cabinet device.
