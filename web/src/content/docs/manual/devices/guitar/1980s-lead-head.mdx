**A design of Solmara's own.**

A modelled British two-channel master-volume guitar head of the late 1980s, without a cabinet: an input valve and its gain pot, a second stage into a pair of diodes (a red LED one way, silicon the other) and the lead master on the lead channel, a rhythm channel that passes them by with its own clip switch for the diodes, a third stage into a cathode follower, a passive tone stack, an output master, and four European output pentodes in push-pull. Input gain, lead master, output master, bass, middle, treble, presence, the channel, and the rhythm clip. It wants a cabinet after it, the speaker cabinet device. Modelled on the British 100-watt lead head of the late 1980s whose diode-clipped lead channel became a rock and metal sound of its own.
