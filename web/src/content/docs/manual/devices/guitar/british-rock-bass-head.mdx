**A design of Solmara's own.**

A modelled British 100-watt valve bass head of the 1970s, without its cabinet: the jumpered head's circuit of two channels and four input jacks, with its own numbers, no master volume, a passive tone stack and four European output pentodes in push-pull, turned up until the power stage distorts. Bright and normal volumes, bass, middle, treble, presence, which jack the bass is in and whether a jumper joins the channels, and a quality switch. It wants a cabinet after it, the speaker cabinet device. Modelled on the British 100-watt bass head as a loud rock trio's bassist played it, every knob up but the bass, a distorted bass that fills the room.
