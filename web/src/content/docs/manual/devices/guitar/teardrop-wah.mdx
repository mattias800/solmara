**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1960s inductor wah pedal.

An inductor wah pedal for guitar, keys and bass: a resonant peak that the pedal sweeps from some 480 Hz at the heel to 2 kHz at the toe, the lows held under it. The pedal is a parameter to automate or to map a MIDI expression pedal to; or an envelope follower or an oscillator moves it, as an auto-wah. Pedal, mode, depth, rate, sensitivity, and an output. Modelled on the American wah of the late 1960s.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Teardrop Wah. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Clav Quack**: The envelope on a clav or an electric piano, kept quick and sensitive.
- **Funk Auto-Wah**: The envelope moves it from the heel: each strum opens it and lets it fall back.
- **Parked Honk**: Parked three quarters open: the nasal, cutting honk of a lead with the wah left on.
- **Rock Pedal**: The pedal by itself, half way: automate it, or map an expression pedal to it, and rock it.
- **Slow Sweep**: The oscillator rocks it slowly over most of its travel, for a chord left ringing.
