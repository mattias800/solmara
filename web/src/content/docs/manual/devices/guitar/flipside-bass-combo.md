**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the 1960s American flip-top valve bass combo of the studios.

A modelled 1960s flip-top bass combo with its sealed 1x15: two channels, the 1964 preamp and the 1966 one, each two valve stages with a volume and a passive bass and treble, into a small push-pull pair of beam tetrodes on a valve rectifier that sags, with no master. Volume, bass, and treble, the channel, the jack, the bias of either year, and the cabinet on or off. Round and soft, the studio and soul bass sound: modelled on the flip-top combo of the 1960s and fitted to a plugin model of the unit.
