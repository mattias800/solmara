**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on a modern metal rig: a gate, a chug, low dirt, a tight amp and a cabinet.

A modern metal guitar rig in one device, from a DI to a miked cabinet, each section switched by itself: a gate; chug, which cuts the lows for a moment after each pick so palm mutes stay tight; low dirt, a hard-clipped fuzz of the lows added under the guitar; a high-gain amp that evens out the level it is given, clips hard after a band-pass round 1.4 kHz, with drive, low, mid, high, and presence; a lo-fi band-pass; its own cabinet; low and high cuts; and mono or stereo. It holds the audio back by 32 frames.

[Chug Rig on the website](/solmara/guitar/#chug-rig)
