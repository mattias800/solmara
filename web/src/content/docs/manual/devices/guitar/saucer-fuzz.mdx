**An emulation of an analogue original,** modelled on the late-1960s British round two-transistor fuzz.

A two-transistor fuzz for guitar, germanium or silicon: two transistors in common emitter fed back on each other, clipping hard and lopsided into a thick, buzzing sustain that cleans up as the guitar is turned down. Germanium is rounder and darker with a thinner bass at full fuzz; silicon has more gain and a brighter, more even edge. Fuzz, volume, the transistors, and an output; light or full quality. Modelled on the round fuzz pedal of the late 1960s and fitted to a model of it.

## Presets

5 factory presets come with Saucer Fuzz. Click the device's name to browse them, and try one while the song plays; the library has them too. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Cleans Up**: Germanium with the fuzz part way: dig in for fuzz, play softly or turn the guitar down for a rough crunch.
- **Fuzz Bass**: Germanium at full quality on a bass: fuzz on the notes, less folding back from the highs.
- **Silicon Grit**: Silicon at half: a gritty rhythm fuzz that keeps the chords apart.
- **Silicon Lead**: Silicon, full up: more gain and a brighter, more even edge for a singing lead.
- **Sixties Wall**: Germanium, the fuzz full up: the thick, lopsided buzz of the late sixties, its bass thinned a little.
