**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the early-1980s Japanese analog rhythm composer whose kick and handclap made electro and hip-hop.

The analog drum machine of the early 1980s as one instrument: its sixteen sounds on their General MIDI keys, the bridged-T kick, the snare, three toms and three congas, the rim shot and the claves, the hand clap and the maracas, the cowbell, the cymbal and the two hats, the closed one cutting the open; each sound's own knobs, the machine's accent and its volume. It expands into a drum rack of its voices, for an effect on one sound.
