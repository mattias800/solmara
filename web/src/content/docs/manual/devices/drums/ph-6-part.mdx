**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on one voice of the early-1980s Japanese small analog drum machine.

One channel of the PH-6 alone, for a pad of a drum rack: the tabs on its face choose which, the bass drum, snare, low and high toms, cymbal and hats, each keeping its own knobs; any key strikes it.

## Appendix: the factory presets

PH-6 Part comes with 32 factory presets. Click the device's name to browse them, each with a line on what it is, and try one while the song plays. See [presets](/solmara/manual/guides/devices/#presets).

- **Bass drum:** Click Kick, High Kick, Low Kick, Machine Kick, Pillow Kick, Ringing Thump, Short Thump, Soft Kick.
- **Closed hat:** Loose Hat, Machine Closed Hat, Tick Hat.
- **Cymbal:** Dark Ride, Machine Cymbal.
- **High tom:** Low Bongo, Machine High Tom, Rack Tom, Short High Tom.
- **Low tom:** Deep Tom, Floor Tom, Machine Low Tom, Short Low Tom.
- **Open hat:** Long Open Hat, Machine Open Hat, Short Open Hat.
- **Snare drum:** Body Snare, High Snare, Low Snare, Machine Snare, Noise Burst, Snappy Snare, Tight Snare, Washy Snare.
