**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the early-1980s Japanese small analog drum machine, the bass machine's companion.

The small analog drum machine of the early 1980s as one instrument: its seven sounds on their General MIDI keys, the kick, the snare, the low and high toms, the cymbal and the open and closed hats, the closed one cutting the open; each sound's own knobs, the machine's accent and its volume. It expands into a drum rack of its voices, for an effect on one sound.
