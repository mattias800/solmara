**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the 1960s American valve optical leveler, the studio's vocal compressor.

A modeled optical leveling amplifier: a valve amplifier whose input a photoresistor turns down as a light driven by the audio brightens. Two knobs, how much it turns down and how loud it comes out, a switch from compressing to limiting, and an emphasis that has it level the highs more; threshold, ratio, attack, and release are not set but come of the light and the cell. Modeled on the two-knob valve leveler of the 1960s that studios put on vocals and bass: slow, smooth, and forgiving. It takes far more processing than the compressor.
