**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the 1980s Danish valve optical compressor of mixing and mastering rooms.

A modeled optical compressor with a valve make-up amplifier: a photoresistor lit by the sidechain turns the audio down ahead of the amplifier. Threshold, ratio, attack, release, and gain, with fixed, manual, or combined timing, and a switch that takes the valves out for a clean amplifier at a fraction of the processing. Modeled on the blue-faced Danish valve opto compressor of the 1980s, a usual choice on vocals, bass, and a mix bus for gain reduction that is hard to hear.
