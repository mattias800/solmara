**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the bus compressor of the 1980s British large-format consoles.

A modeled bus compressor built on a voltage-controlled amplifier, for gluing a mix bus or a drum bus: a threshold, ratios of 2, 4, and 10 to 1, an attack of 0.1 to 30 ms and a release of 0.1 to 1.2 s on switches, with an automatic release that lets a short peak go quickly and a long passage slowly, and a make-up gain. One peak detector serves both channels, so the stereo image holds still. Its timing is the circuit's network of a diode, resistances, and capacitors, solved exactly. With a high-pass in the sidechain, a mix of the compressed with what went in, a switch for where the detector listens, ahead of the gain element or behind it, and a sidechain from another track. Modeled on the stereo compressor in the center section of a large British mixing console of the 1980s.
