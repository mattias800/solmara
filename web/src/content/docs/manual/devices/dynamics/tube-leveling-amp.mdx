**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the 1990s American valve leveling amplifier.

A modeled American valve leveling amplifier of the 1990s: one knob sets how much and how hard, its ratio growing with the reduction; three attacks and releases, the slower ones following the program; a valve after the cell whose headroom the saturation knob moves, a low cut on the input or the detector, a parallel mix. At four times the sample rate.
