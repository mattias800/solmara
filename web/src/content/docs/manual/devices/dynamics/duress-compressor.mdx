**An emulation of an analog original,** modeled on the 1990s American compressor that imitates valves and tape with analog circuits under digital control.

A modeled character compressor for vocals, drums, bass and rooms: an input knob that drives a fixed threshold and an output after it, attacks from under a tenth of a millisecond to some 40 ms and releases from a twentieth of a second to several, ratios of 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 10 and 20 to 1, each with its own knee, and Nuke, a brickwall; a detector that can hear less of the low end or more of the sibilant band, and a stereo link; a high-pass in the audio; and two harmonic generators, Dist 2, a second harmonic that grows with the reduction, and Dist 3, a clipping stage driven harder for a third. A mix for parallel compression. Modeled on the American digitally controlled analog compressors of the middle 1990s.
