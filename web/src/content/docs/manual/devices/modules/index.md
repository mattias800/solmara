The [Modular](/solmara/manual/guides/modular/) has 75 modules in 11 groups. Each group has a page: what each module does, its jacks, and its knobs with their ranges and where they start.

## Jacks

Inputs are on the left of a module and outputs on the right. A jack carries a voltage, and its kind says what the voltage means:

- **audio:** a sound, ten volts from peak to peak at full scale.
- **control:** a voltage that moves something, slowly or as fast as audio.
- **gate:** on at 10 V or off at 0 V; a short gate is a trigger.
- **clock:** pulses that keep time.
- **pitch:** one volt an octave, with 0 V at middle C.

The kinds are a guide, not a rule: any output can go into any input.

Every knob also has an input of its own. A cable into it moves the knob from where it is set, so a patch is still played with its knobs. The group pages list only a module's own jacks.

## The groups

- **[Sources](./sources/)**: Modular Oscillator, Complex Oscillator, Morph Oscillator, Noise and Random, Grain Texture, Character Oscillator, Wavetable Oscillator, Sample Oscillator, Multisample Oscillator.
- **[Filters and amps](./filters-and-amps/)**: Modular VCA, Modular Multimode Filter, Dual Multimode Filter, Modal Resonator, Spectral Bank, Coupled Low-pass Gate, Character Filter.
- **[Shapers](./shapers/)**: Wavefolder, Ring Modulator, Voltage Saturator, Shaper, Voltage Rectifier.
- **[Modulation](./modulation/)**: Modular LFO, Modular ADSR, Slope Function, Stepped Random, Sample and Hold, Envelope Follower, Probability Gates, Phasor.
- **[Sequencing](./sequencing/)**: Modular Clock, Clock Divider, Voltage Sequencer, Euclidean Gates, Trigger Delay, Transport, Step Sequencer, Trigger Sequencer, Clock Ratio, Clock Quantizer, Ratchet, Shift Register.
- **[Effects](./effects/)**: Modular Delay, Chorus / Flanger, Phaser, Frequency Shifter, Pitch Shifter.
- **[Logic and math](./logic-and-math/)**: Gate Logic, Voltage Comparator, Math, Latch, Counter.
- **[Mixing and routing](./mixing-and-routing/)**: Modular Mixer, Sequential Switch, Router, Mid/Side, Crossover, Stereo Mixer, Voltage Crossfader, Voltage Panner, Buffered Multiple.
- **[Utilities](./utilities/)**: Attenuverter, Constant Voltage, Slew Limiter, Pitch Quantizer, Pitch Utility, Note to CV, Voice Info.
- **[Displays](./displays/)**: Value Readout, XY Scope, Spectrum, Label, Signal Monitor.
- **[Subpatches](./subpatches/)**: Subpatch, Subpatch Input, Subpatch Output.
