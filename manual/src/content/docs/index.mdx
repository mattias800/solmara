Solmara is a digital audio workstation: you play, record and arrange music in it, shape the sound with instruments and effects, and mix and master the result. This manual shows you how.

<Aside type="caution" title="Solmara is early">
Solmara is in active development and changes from week to week. This manual is new too: the pages below are the first ones, and more are on the way.
</Aside>

## What the manual will cover

- **Learn:** your first beat in fifteen minutes; record and arrange a song.
- **The arrangement:** tracks, clips, automation, markers, the tempo and time signature.
- **Writing notes:** the piano roll, the Composer window, chords and sequences, the drum and step editors, patterns.
- **Recording:** audio and MIDI, takes and comping.
- **Instruments and effects:** the device chain, the built-in instruments and effects, racks, the Modular, plugins.
- **The library:** presets, samples, and getting more content.
- **Mixing and mastering:** the mixer, sends and groups, the mastering suite.
- **Preferences** and **keyboard shortcuts**.
